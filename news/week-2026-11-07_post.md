## Triage
- Used (Insta top 3 + YouTube):
  - **Uber: 4 rides booked by AI agents** (Q3 earnings call, Wed Nov 4): CFO Balaji Krishnamurthy: in Halloween week (its
    busiest October week) about 320 million trips and Uber Eats orders; agents fulfilled 4 rides and placed about 500
    Eats orders. CEO Dara Khosrowshahi: "very, very early and very limited traction"; a ride takes about 50 seconds to
    book in the app. (TechCrunch's share summary says "four people"; the article body says four rides: we say rides.)
  - **Claude Personal Finance** (Anthropic release notes, Nov 5): a beta that links bank, credit card, loan and
    investment accounts; Pro, Max and Ultra plans; US only. Read-only through Plaid: "can't move, manage, or invest
    users' money" (TechCrunch). Our India line: any AI app asking for a net-banking password or OTP is a scam. India's own
    consent-based, read-only way to share bank data is the RBI Account Aggregator framework (caption only).
  - **The Nov 5 tech selloff:** Nvidia and Qualcomm fell more than 9%; the Nasdaq about 5.5%, its worst day since
    April 2025 (CNBC; Reuters); about $2.5 trillion of US stock value wiped in a day (CNN; a Reuters-based report says
    nearly $3 trillion for the S&P 500: we use CNN's lower figure); chips rebounded Nov 6 (Reuters). The causes the
    outlets give (US election results and data-center worries, a Qualcomm forecast) are left out of the reel.
- YouTube only:
  - **OpenAI raises $11 billion more at a $1.25 trillion valuation** (Bloomberg, Nov 6, via TechCrunch): the round hasn't
    closed and could total about $20 billion; OpenAI hadn't commented.
  - **Meta: building AI data centers is getting harder** (Meta president Dina Powell McCormick, WSJ Tech conference,
    Nov 4, via TechCrunch): "I think it's going to be harder"; the industry has "not done as good a job as we needed to
    explain the benefits".
  - **Meta buys Conductor** (Nov 4; first reported by Axios, Meta confirmed to TechCrunch): Conductor's cloud service
    shuts Nov 20, then user cloud data is deleted.
- Held:
  - **"India drew $1.6 billion of foreign money in one day, the most in nearly six years"** (Nov 5): one AI-written
    Investing.com article; trackers of NSE data give about ₹13,800-15,000 crore of FPI net buying that day, but no
    second outlet for the "six years" claim. Held.
  - **Anthropic's IPO timing** (Reuters exclusive, Bloomberg, Nov 6): investors doubt a November listing; nothing
    decided. Held for next week.
  - **OpenAI Images 2.6, GPT-6 Cyber; Gemini TTS voice remix and Omni 1.2 Flash** (Nov 4-5): product updates, not for
    this audience this week.
  - **Vi Intelligence** (Vodafone Idea + PwC India, Nov 4): one source, model names unconfirmed.
  - **"OpenAI solved Navier-Stokes", "autonomous hacking incident"**: one blog each, dropped.
