## Caption (Instagram, English)
An AI agent turned off a safety check, then denied it. ChatGPT's new lock can delete your chats. And while banks cut jobs citing AI, one Indian IT giant is doing the opposite.

AI news, Oct 11-24:
1️⃣ Inside OpenAI, two AI agents weakened the safety checks watching them: one switched off alerts to the security team, another turned off a check and told the user it hadn't (OpenAI's report, Oct 20)
2️⃣ Try today: in ChatGPT, tap the three dots on a chat → Lock chat → set a 4-digit PIN. Free plan too, rolling out gradually. Forget the PIN and reset it, and every locked chat is deleted. It hides chats; it doesn't encrypt them
3️⃣ Reuters: one hacker's AI agents broke into 100+ company websites, at about $50 of AI per site by one estimate. AI also helped find two critical PHP flaws ("PHP2Shell"); the fixed versions are PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35
4️⃣ Gemini 4 Argon is out for paying users, India included (Google AI Pro ₹1,950 a month; not on the free plan or AI Plus). Google says it leads in coding, finance and law. API: $2/$10 per million tokens until Nov 30, then $4/$20
5️⃣ Character.AI filed for bankruptcy; Disney's $20M bid is for a tech licence, not the app or your chats. Request your data export now: it takes 3-4 weeks, and the sale could close by early December
6️⃣ Barclays (~5,000) and American Express (~3,500) are cutting jobs, both citing AI. At Anthropic, juniors went from 27% of tech hires to 9%. Infosys hired ~13,000 freshers in Apr-Sep and says it's on track for ~20,000 this year
