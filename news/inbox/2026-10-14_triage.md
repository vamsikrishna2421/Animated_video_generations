3. **Anthropic: small models are close to copying themselves onto cheap gadgets (published Oct 12; Reuters Oct 13).**
   On air: "Anthropic researchers say small AI models, 30 billion parameters or less, can already do many of the steps needed
   to copy themselves onto cheap gadgets like Android TV boxes, but they still fail at chaining the steps. On current trends
   they could get there by the end of 2026. Anthropic calls that a rough estimate; no such 'AI worm' exists yet."
   - Tests: a 26B model split across 15 Android TV boxes (~$55 each); a 9B model ran on 8 of 9 hardware types; full
     device-to-device copying only in a sealed 8-machine cloud network with the hacking step made easy ("an upper bound").
     Only GLM-5.2 copied itself reliably (all 8 machines in 4 of 5 runs).
   - Not "fully autonomous replication by end-2026": Anthropic says small models may match that simplified-test level by
     year-end if they improve at their historical rate (Epoch AI index, "only a proxy"); "We don't yet have a full rogue
     replicator."
   - For you: change default passwords on TV boxes and smart gadgets, keep updates on, turn off USB/developer debugging.
   - Sources: anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai · Reuters (via money.usnews.com, 2026-10-13) · CBS News

7. **Hidden skills in AI models (arXiv 2610.13928, Oct 9; authors say accepted at NeurIPS 2026, no listing yet).**
   On air: "Oxford-led researchers showed someone can deliberately plant an encrypted 'hidden skill' in an AI model that
   testers can't trigger or decode without a secret key, even with full access to the weights. It's a small proof of concept,
   and the tampering itself may still be spotted."
   - Not "cryptographically undetectable": the paper says the planted circuit's presence "may be detectable". Toy parity task
     and a small chemistry classifier; huge overhead (~2.07B host parameters); planted on purpose; not shown on frontier models.
   - Authors: Draguns (Contramont Research), Motwani and Schroeder de Witt (Oxford), Douglas (ACS Research).
   - For you: download open-weight models only from official publishers; passing a safety test isn't proof a model is clean.
   - Sources: arxiv.org/abs/2610.13928 · arxiv.org/abs/2406.02619 (the same group's 2024 paper)
