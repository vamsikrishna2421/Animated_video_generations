## Triage
- Used (Insta top 3 + YouTube):
  - **Notion AI chats exposed in a breach at its vendor Fireworks AI** (Notion's advisory; Fireworks confirmed customer
    data was accessed on Nov 8): AI chat transcripts from Notion's Insights feature, from under 1% of workspaces, most
    without user or workspace details; Insights is now off. Fireworks had a contractual zero-data-retention commitment, but
    data from the endpoints Notion used was kept. Notion's advice: treat passwords and API keys pasted into Notion AI as
    exposed, and change them. (Notion's own help centre says its enterprise ZDR never covered chat history or Insights.)
  - **ChatGPT Wallet** (OpenAI, Nov 13): ChatGPT's agents can complete purchases; every purchase needs a biometric check
    (Face ID or a fingerprint); a tokenized card, so the merchant never sees the card number; US Pro subscribers, 18+,
    eligible US cards only; spending limits planned, no date.
  - **Nvidia's CEO resigns** (Thu Nov 12): Jensen Huang, who co-founded Nvidia in 1993, resigned as president, CEO and
    director, effective immediately; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO (Nvidia's 8-K). The stock dived as much as 16%
    intraday and closed down 4.3% (NBC News); on Nov 13 Nvidia closed under a $4 trillion market value for the first time
    since July 2025 (CNBC). The reported reasons (personal allegations) are left out of the reel.
- YouTube only:
  - **Businesses in ChatGPT** (OpenAI, Nov 10, beta): Business Hub to claim and verify a listing; Business Agents for
    eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses; OpenAI says businesses don't get users' conversations.
  - **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** (Google, Nov 12): a fast, lower-cost model in AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business,
    the Gemini app and Search; free app users get it through automatic model selection; $1.50 / $7.50 per million
    input / output tokens.
  - **Anthropic's IPO range** (an updated SEC filing, Nov 10): 825 million Class A shares at $70-78, Nasdaq "ANT";
    pricing expected Nov 18 and trading Nov 19 (CNBC, people familiar); up to $1.404 trillion fully diluted at $78
    (TechCrunch).
- Held / dropped:
  - **The $129 million deepfake-CEO fraud charges** (DOJ, announced Nov 5) and **IndiaAI's EKA-1 and Dhīr models**
    (launched Nov 3): outside this week's window (Nov 7-13) and not in Weekly 04.
  - **"Nearly 20% of US workers replaced by AI are being asked back"** (Fortune, Nov 9, an SHL survey): one outlet, a
    vendor survey; held.
  - **Tech layoffs past 200,000 in 2026** (TrueUp tracker): a running total, not this week's news.
  - **Fireworks' second intrusion, Grok Build share links, United Airlines and AI agents**: single sources or too niche.
