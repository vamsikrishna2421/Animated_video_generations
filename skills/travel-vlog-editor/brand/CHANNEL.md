# Channel notes: The Travellers (@thetravellers7066)

Read this at the start of every video. Append what you learn after each one.

## Snapshot (2026-10-05)
- 62 subscribers, 18 videos, ~10.3k total views. Created 2020; long-form, mostly 8-25 min.
- Spoken language: **Telugu**, with English place/transit words. No subtitles so far.
- Locations so far: Massachusetts / New England (Worcester, Boston, Rhode Island, apple farms, hikes, museum).

## What works
- Best video by far: **"Navigating Boston: South Station to Logan Airport"** (544 views, 4 comments), about 10x the
  scenic vlogs (27-87 views). It is a practical, step-by-step guide for Telugu-speaking students and newcomers.
  Lesson: **useful guides in Telugu are the channel's edge.** Make more of them; title them with the exact route/place.
- Second: "Tougas Apple Farm | Pumpkin Chronicles..." (360 views): a specific, seasonal, named place.

## What to improve (from watching the videos)
- No hook: videos start with the plan/intro. Open on the best moment or the problem + promise.
- No on-screen help: add step badges, route map, prices, location tags, a recap card.
- Long silent walking stretches (20 s+) and dead air: speed up or cut, add an arrow/step card.
- Audio: raw camera sound only, no music; fluctuating levels. Add licensed music under b-roll, duck under speech,
  normalise loudness.
- Ending: a plain "Thanks for watching" text. Use the 20 s end screen with two video slots + subscribe.
- Unreadable phone screens (app tickets): re-create the key info as a `tip` / `counter` card.
- Add English subtitles (SRT) so non-Telugu viewers and search can use the guides.
