"The animal didn't cross the street because it was too tired." You know "it" is the animal. Change one word to "wide" and "it" becomes the street. How does an AI know?

Episode 27 of AI From Scratch: Attention, the key idea behind ChatGPT
- Old models read one word at a time, and the start of a sentence goes blurry
- Attention: each word looks back at the earlier words and picks what matters
- How it picks: a question, a name tag, borrowed meaning (query, key, value)
- The catch: double your prompt and the attention work roughly quadruples. That's why long chats get slow and have limits
- 2017: Google built a model entirely from attention, the Transformer. The T in ChatGPT
