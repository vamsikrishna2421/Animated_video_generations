Episode 48 of AI From Scratch: AI laws (India, the EU and the rest)
- India: under the IT Rules as amended in 2026 (in force 20 Feb 2026), AI-made or AI-changed video, photos and audio that look real must be clearly and prominently labelled. Big platforms (over 5 million users in India) must ask you whether an upload is AI-made, check it, and label it. AI-written text isn't covered. Unlawful content: taken down within 3 hours of a court order or government notice (it was 36)
- Europe: the EU AI Act (2024) has banned some uses since 2 Feb 2025, like AI that reads people's emotions at work or in school, and "social scoring". Its rules for high-risk uses (hiring, loans, exams) were moved this July: now from 2 Dec 2027 (it was 2 Aug 2026)
- Our test: we asked a small free AI from 2024 (no internet) about these rules, 25 times, and read every reply. It got 2 right. 8 times out of 10 it said India needs no label (the rule came after it was made). It said takedown is 24 hours (the rule: 3), that Europe's hiring-AI rules started "May 25, 2024" (never true), and that Europe doesn't ban AI that reads workers' emotions (the ban was already in the EU's 2024 law; it applies since 2 Feb 2025)
- Some answers were old. Some were just wrong, in a confident voice

What to do:
1. A question about a rule or a law? Use an AI app that searches the web, and make it show the government's own page and its date
2. Posting an AI video, photo or voice that looks real? Label it
3. Every answer has a date, this video too: November 2026
