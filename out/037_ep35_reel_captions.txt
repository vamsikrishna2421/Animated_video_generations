Episode 35 of AI From Scratch: model size and small language models
- A model's size is its parameters, the numbers it's made of: our big one has 7 billion (a 4.7 GB file), the medium one 1.5 billion (1.1 GB), the tiny one half a billion (0.5 GB)
- Brains small enough for a phone are called small language models (Meta's Llama 3.2 1B and 3B, Google's Gemma 3 1B, Microsoft's Phi-3-mini, Apple's on-device model)
- Our real test, 40 shop WhatsApp messages sorted into order / payment / complaint / other: tiny 14 right, medium 39, big 39, and the medium one was 4x faster than the big one
- On 20 bills the same files made 19, 9 and 2 mistakes

How to use this:
1. Give each job a pass mark first: sorting messages can live with one miss; a bill can't
2. Try the sizes on 20 to 40 of your own examples and keep the smallest that passes
3. For money, no model passed: use a calculator (or a big rented model, tested, EP34)

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
How we tested (run by us): 40 WhatsApp-style shop messages ("anna 2 kg rice and 1 litre oil pls", "sent 450 on UPI, pls check", "the milk you sent yesterday was sour", "are you open on Sunday?"), the right tray written down first. Three sizes of one free model family (Qwen2.5-Instruct, Apache 2.0), each as Qwen's own 4-bit file, run with llama.cpp on a laptop CPU (4 threads, no graphics card). Right: 14, 39, 39 of 40, at about 0.2, 0.5 and 2 seconds a message. The tiny one filed "happy diwali anna and family" and "come home, lunch is ready" as orders. The medium and big ones both missed "QR code not working, what is your UPI id?".
Bills: EP34's 20-order test on the same 4-bit files: 1, 11 and 18 right (19, 9 and 2 wrong). In EP34 the two small ones ran from their larger 16-bit downloads (1.0 and 3.1 GB) and got 1 and 9.
Honest limits: 40 messages is a small demo, not a benchmark. Our speeds are a laptop's; a phone's will differ. To run, a model also needs about its file size in free memory (RAM), not just storage. Rented (closed) models come in sizes too, and the small ones cost less per question.
Small models made for phones: Meta, "Llama 3.2" (Sept 25, 2024): 1B and 3B models "that fit onto edge and mobile devices"; Microsoft, Phi-3 technical report (Apr 2024): Phi-3-mini, 3.8 billion parameters, "small enough to be deployed on a phone"; Apple's foundation-model reports (2024, 2025): an on-device model of about 3 billion parameters; Google Developers Blog (2025): Gemma 3 1B runs on a phone's CPU or GPU.
