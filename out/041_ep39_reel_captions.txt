17 seconds for one WhatsApp reply. The customer cancelled.

Episode 39 of AI From Scratch: speed, latency and cost per token
- Every AI reply is made of tokens: a word, or a piece of one ("sun·flower" is 2 tokens, "302" is 3)
- Two clocks: first the AI reads your whole message (the wait before the first word: latency), then it writes one token at a time
- Our real test, internet cut, on a 4-core computer with no graphics card, the squeezed 7B brain from EP37-38: 4.3 s to read the order, then 4.3 tokens a second: 17.6 s for a 58-token reply
- Online AIs run on big graphics cards: Gemini 2.5 Flash writes about 200 tokens a second (Artificial Analysis). But a shop's bot pays for every token, and a token it writes usually costs 4-8x a token you send (OpenAI and Google price lists): 1,000 replies like ours cost about ₹10-16

What this means for you:
1. Want faster (and, online, cheaper) replies? Ask for short ones: "Reply in 2 short lines." Our test: 58 → 35 tokens, 17.6 → 11.7 s
2. Don't make it read what it doesn't need. A month of old chat (1,788 tokens) made our AI wait 92 s before its first word; online, you'd pay for that month again on every reply. To remember a regular customer, give it a 2-line note, not the whole chat
3. In ChatGPT: the plans aren't billed per token, but long answers and long chats still take longer. New topic? New chat
4. Thinking models also bill their hidden thinking as output tokens, so a short answer can still cost more
5. Telugu costs more tokens: our message took 69 tokens in Telugu vs 37 in English on GPT-4o's tokenizer, and 184 vs 41 on our small open model's

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
How we tested (run by us): Qwen's own 4-bit GGUF files (Q4_K_M) of Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and 1.5B-Instruct, llama.cpp on a 4-core computer (server CPU, no graphics card), greedy, the reply streamed token by token, 3 runs per test (the median kept), the internet cut for every run. 7B: first token after 4.26 s, whole reply 17.62 s (58 tokens); with "Reply in 2 short lines.": 11.65 s (35 tokens); with a month of old chat (30 orders, 1,788 tokens) in front: first token after 91.83 s. The small 1.5B brain wrote the short reply in 2.15 s, but dropped the quantities.
"So should the shop use an online AI?" Online is far faster (about a second for a reply like this) and costs about ₹10-16 per 1,000 replies at Gemini 2.5 Flash or GPT-4.1 mini prices (and a month of chat in front makes every reply about 4-6x dearer); your customers' messages go to that company. The laptop costs nothing per reply and stays private, but it is slow. Either way: short replies.
Prices per million tokens, input → output (checked Oct 26, 2026): Gemini 2.5 Flash $0.30 → $2.50; GPT-4.1 mini $0.40 → $1.60; GPT-5 mini $0.25 → $2.00 (a thinking model). Speed: Artificial Analysis, Gemini 2.5 Flash on Google's API, 198 tokens a second, first token after 0.37 s.
