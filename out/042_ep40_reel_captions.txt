Episode 40 of AI From Scratch: benchmarks, how AI models are compared
- A benchmark is one exam paper, the same for every AI, with known answers. The "maths 73.2%" on Qwen2.5-1.5B's chart is GSM8K: 1,319 school maths word problems (OpenAI, 2021)
- A score belongs to one brain, tested one way. The 73.2 is the maker's own test of its full-size brain. Our real test, internet cut: the same small brain, squeezed to 4-bit the way people run it on a laptop, our prompt, the first 30 questions of that paper: 18 of 30, 60%
- Copying is a real worry: the paper has been online since 2021, and exam questions can leak into what AIs learn from (contamination). Scale AI wrote a fresh copy of this exam and found some model families dropping by up to 8%. So we checked ours: the same 30 questions with only the names and numbers changed ("Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs" → "Lakshmi's ducks lay 22 eggs"): 20 of 30, 67%. No drop, no sign of copying
- On Ravi's own test (20 real bills against his price board, the right totals written down first): 11 of 20 for the 1.1 GB brain, 15 for the 2.1 GB, 18 for the 4.7 GB. The chart's order held. Only his own test showed the 9 wrong bills

What this means for you:
1. Before you pick or pay for an AI, test it on your own work: 10-20 real tasks, the right answers written down before you start, the same test for every AI
2. Test the exact version and settings you'll use (a free tier, a small or squeezed model, your own prompt), not the one on the chart
3. Read a launch chart as "how it did on that exam, run the maker's way". Useful for the order, not for your score
4. Keep your test to yourself. A test posted online can end up in the next AIs' training data, and then it measures memory, not skill

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
How we tested (run by us): Qwen's own 4-bit GGUF file (Q4_K_M) of Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, llama.cpp on a 4-core computer (server CPU, no graphics card), greedy, the internet cut for every run. The first 30 questions of GSM8K's test set, word for word, each with "Solve it step by step, then write the final answer on its own line as: Answer: <number>"; then the same 30 with only the names and numbers changed (new answers worked out by us). Every reply was read by hand: 18 of 30 on the paper, 20 of 30 on the changed copy. The bill test is EP38's: 20 orders against the shop's 15-line price board, exact totals.
Why 60% and not 73.2%? The maker tested the full-size brain on all 1,319 questions, its own way; ours is the squeezed file, 30 questions, our prompt. Different copy, different test.
Sources: Qwen2.5 blog and technical report (qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen2.5-llm, arXiv 2412.15115), GSM8K (OpenAI, arXiv 2110.14168), GSM1k (Scale AI, arXiv 2405.00332), GSM-Symbolic (Apple, arXiv 2410.05229).
