Episode 52 of AI From Scratch: build RAG over your PDFs, Part 1 (Season 6, a screen tutorial)
1. Read the PDF page by page (3 lines of Python, pypdf).
2. For a short PDF, don't search: send every page, each marked "--- page N ---". Our first try searched for "the best page" and picked the cover 9 times out of 10: every question said "MG-750", and only the cover did too. Five pages are about 450 tokens a question. "Short" means the whole PDF fits the AI's limit with room to spare; too big to send with every question, it needs a search by meaning (Part 2). No code? Paste the pages into any AI chat, each marked page 1, page 2.
3. The rules: answer only from the document, say the page, and if it isn't there, say "Not in the document".

Our test, a 5-page warranty card we wrote for a made-up mixer, ten questions, every reply read by hand (a small test, on one small free model on our computer):
- no PDF: 0 of 10
- the first search (it read the cover): 1 of 10
- all 5 pages, numbered: 9 of 10 (the right page on 7, a wrong page on 1, and 1 rightly "not in the document")
The miss: "I ran it 10 minutes non-stop, is it covered?" It said "Not in the document"; page 4 says it isn't covered. So open the page it gives, every time; and when it says "not there", check the PDF yourself.

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
The code (Python; pypdf's PdfReader and page.extract_text()):
from pypdf import PdfReader
reader = PdfReader("warranty.pdf")
pages = [p.extract_text() for p in reader.pages]
doc = "".join(f"--- page {i+1} ---\n{t}\n" for i, t in enumerate(pages))
rules = ("Answer only from the document. Say the page. If it isn't there, say: Not in the document.")
messages = [{"role": "system", "content": rules}, {"role": "user", "content": doc + question}]
How we tested (Nov 13, 2026): a 5-page warranty card for a made-up "MG-750" mixer grinder (Sample Appliances; motor 5 years, jars 1 year, coupler 6 months; motor rewinding Rs 1,450 outside the warranty), Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (4-bit) on llama.cpp's OpenAI-compatible server, temperature 0.2, ten questions with the answers fixed before the run, every reply read by hand. The first search counted shared words (it picked the cover 9 of 10 times); counting rare words more didn't help ("MG-750" is only on the cover, so it counts as rare). Sending all 5 pages: about 450 prompt tokens a question (our setup gave the AI 4,096 tokens of room; a full page of real text can be 500+ tokens, so count yours); 9 of 10 right; q8 (a dropped jar) right but cited page 3 (it's page 4); the miss, q3, said "Not in the document" though page 4 has it. Pages first, then the question, as Anthropic's prompting guide advises: "Place your long documents and inputs near the top of your prompt, above your query". Bigger models may do better; check the page either way.
