ChatGPT never sees your words. "The Statue of Liberty is in" reaches it as 976 160801 328 51868 382 306. So how do those numbers turn into "New York"?

Episode 28 of AI From Scratch: how AI turns words into numbers (Transformers, Part 2)
- Tokens: your text is cut into words or pieces ("YouTube" = "You" + "Tube"), and each token gets an ID
- Embeddings: each ID fetches a long list of numbers from a giant table, an address on a map of meaning. Google Maps needs 2 numbers; OpenAI's open model gpt-oss uses 2,880
- Position: "dog bites man" vs "man bites dog", so every token also carries its place
- Layers: on every floor, each word looks back and soaks up what matters
- Next token: score every option, pick a likely one ("New"), climb again ("York"). Every new token is another climb, which is why answers appear piece by piece
