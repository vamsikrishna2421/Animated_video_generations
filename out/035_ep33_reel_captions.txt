Episode 33 of AI From Scratch: hybrid search, and how to test your AI search (RAG, part 3)
- Meaning search finds what you meant, but an exact code like E-1203 can look like every other code to it
- Keyword search finds the exact word, but "something for a headache" never says "balm": 0 matches
- Use both (hybrid search): run both searches, merge the two lists, and the AI still reads just 3 pages
- Our real test, 20 orders we knew the answers to: meaning only 15/20, keyword only 13/20, both 18/20
- The two misses weren't search problems: "vessels" and "Ramesh garu's usual" were written nowhere in the stock book. Written in: 20/20

How to use this:
1. Hybrid search is a built-in option in Azure AI Search, Elasticsearch, OpenSearch and Weaviate; in Postgres, pgvector plus full-text search
2. Before you trust an AI search, test it with about 20 questions you already know the answers to, and run the same test after every change
3. Write down what "everyone knows": the AI only finds what's written

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
How we tested (run by us): a made-up 114-line kirana stock book cut into 15 pages of 8 lines, and 20 orders whose right item we knew (4 codes, 4 local names, 8 described in other words, 3 mixed, 1 written nowhere). The AI reads the top 3 pages per order. Meaning search = bge-small-en-v1.5 embeddings, keyword search = BM25, both = reciprocal rank fusion (k = 60). Right page in the 3 read: 15, 13, 18 out of 20; 20/20 after writing the two missing facts in.
Honest limits: with one item per piece instead of pages, meaning search found every code and hybrid added nothing, so it helps most when codes hide inside longer pieces. And 20/20 after fixing the exact two misses proves the fix, not the search: a fair re-test uses new orders. 20 orders is a small demo, not a benchmark.
Why only 3 pages? Reading the whole book for every order is slow and costly; RAG hands the AI a few pieces (EP32).
EP32's manual of full sentences suited meaning search; a stock book of codes and short lines is where it struggles.
