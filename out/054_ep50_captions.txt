Episode 50 of AI From Scratch: your first AI API call (Season 6: build it yourself)
- An app with "AI inside" makes API calls: it sends the customer's message and a secret API key to the AI company's computer, which answers and bills whoever owns the key.
- Our test: we locked our own AI (a free model on our own computer, the same kind of call) with a key. No key: refused (401). One letter wrong: refused (401). The right key: it answered. 20 shop questions, 20 right prices.
- One shop reply was about 400 tokens in and 7 out: about 3 paise at a small OpenAI model's published price (gpt-5.4-mini). 50 replies a day: about ₹47 a month. Cheaper models cost less; some have free tiers.
- The ₹8,600 is a story number. The risk is real: GitGuardian counted 1,275,105 exposed secrets for AI services in its 2026 report (28.6 million new secrets in public GitHub commits in 2025).

What to do:
1. The API key is your ATM PIN: keep it in a locked setting only the app reads (an environment variable), never in code, a photo, a chat or a screenshot
2. Turn on a hard spend limit, not just an alert (OpenAI: the Limits page, "Enforce a hard limit"; alerts only warn)
3. Leaked? Delete the key and make another now; ask the company's support about the bill (no promise)
Only use the ChatGPT app? You don't have an API key; this is for anyone building with an AI API.
