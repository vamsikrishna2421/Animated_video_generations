=== TITLE ===
The Biggest AI Brain Failed Our Shop Test: How to Choose an AI Model (Tested) | AI From Scratch Ep 41 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Ravi anna's small AI gave every WhatsApp order the same reply, "Sure, we'll have those ready for you within 30 minutes.", so he reached for the biggest brain. Wrong question: biggest for which job? Write the pass mark first (OpenAI's own model guide: "Choose acceptance criteria before comparing models"): 19 of 20 orders with every item and quantity right, every reply under 10 seconds.

Our real test, internet cut, on a 4-core computer (Qwen2.5 1.5B, 3B and 7B, 4-bit, as people run them on a laptop), his 20 orders, every reply read by hand: 8 of 20 for the 1.1 GB brain, 19 of 20 for the 2.1 GB (every reply under 5 s), 16 of 20 for the 4.7 GB ("2 curd 2 milk" became "2 liters of curd and 2 liters of milk", and one reply took 11 s). Keep the smallest one that passes: faster, less memory. On his bills the biggest is best (18 of 20): a different job, a different winner. And when the job changes, test again: on orders typed with Telugu words, none of the three passed (5, 14, 12 of 20).

What it means for you: write down what "good enough" means first, give every AI the same real tasks, keep the smallest one that passes, and test again when the job changes. None passes? Change the prompt or the model, then the same test again.

How we tested (run by us): Qwen's own GGUF files, llama.cpp, greedy, every run offline, the pass mark written into the test before any model ran.
Sources: OpenAI, "Model selection"; Anthropic, "Choosing the right model".
