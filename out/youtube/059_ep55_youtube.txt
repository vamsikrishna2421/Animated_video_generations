=== DESCRIPTION ===
We moved our shop bot's order lookup into an MCP server (12 lines, the official Python SDK); the bot finds it with list_tools and calls it. Our test, 10 customers: 1 right. The small AI sent the order number as a number; the server's input said text and refused it (the bot then blamed the customer: "Your order ID is numeric"). The fix: accept a number or text, and show the AI the types: 10 of 10 (our second bot 0 → 7; 3 broken JSON lines). One change on the server then reached the bot with no change to the bot. One app: a plain tool; two or more: a server. (A small test on one small model, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, on our computer; a made-up shop.) Full code in the pinned comments.
