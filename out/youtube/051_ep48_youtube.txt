=== TITLE ===
"No Label Needed," Said the AI. India's Rule Says Yes Since Feb 2026 | AI From Scratch Ep 48 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Ravi asked his shop AI if his AI-made ad needs a label. It said no. Since 20 Feb 2026, India's amended IT Rules say AI-made video, photos and audio that look real must be clearly labelled, and big platforms must ask you whether an upload is AI-made.

Our test: a small free AI from 2024 (no internet), asked about AI rules 25 times, every reply read by hand: 2 right. Some answers were old (the rule came after it), some were just wrong (it said Europe doesn't ban AI that reads workers' emotions; the EU AI Act has, since 2 Feb 2025).

For rules and laws: use an AI app that searches the web, and make it show the government's own page and its date. Every answer has a date: this video is from November 2026.

Sources: IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026 (AZB & Partners, Freshfields, Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer summaries); the European Commission's AI Act page; Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus). Not legal advice.
