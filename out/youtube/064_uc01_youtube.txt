=== TITLE ===
42 Seconds of Silence: How an AI Phone Assistant Works | AI From Scratch UC01 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
A phone AI does three steps every turn: ears (voice → text), brain (writes the reply), mouth (text → speech). On our small computer, no GPU, waiting for the whole reply meant 41.5 s of silence; people leave about 0.2 s. Shorter answers: 7 s but the total wrong every time. A smaller model: right once in six. Talking while thinking (say the first sentence while the rest is written): about 2.5 s, total right every time. OpenAI's figures for big apps: 2.8-5.4 s for the old three-step voice mode, 0.32 s for GPT-4o.
