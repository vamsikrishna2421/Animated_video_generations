=== TITLE ===
₹1,299 or ₹0? Build an AI Agent with Tools | AI From Scratch Ep 54 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
A made-up shop's bot told a customer "your refund of Rs 1,299 has been processed". The shop's record: ₹0, not started. It had no way to look. The simplest agent, one tool: describe get_order and ask for a one-line JSON reply; your code finds that line and looks the order up (the AI runs nothing); send the result back, and it answers from the record. Our test, 10 customers: without the tool 10 answers, no checking, 6 wrong; with the tool 10 lookups, 10 right. (A small test on one small model, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, on our computer.)

Next: your own MCP server.
