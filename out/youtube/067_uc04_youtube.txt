=== TITLE ===
1 Video, Then 9 of 10 Trading Videos: How Up Next Picks Your Videos | AI From Scratch UC04 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
To a video app, every video is a list of numbers, a point on a map; alike videos sit close. Up next shows the videos closest to the one playing; your home page is built from your whole history. In our test (a small recommender we built, 144 made-up videos), one trading video put 9 trading videos in the next 10, autoplay played 8 of 10 trading, yet the home page held at 2 of 10. A scam-warning video's Up next was 9 of 10 trading tips too (ours matches titles; real apps may differ). Switch autoplay off, and tap ⋮ → Not interested right in Up next.
