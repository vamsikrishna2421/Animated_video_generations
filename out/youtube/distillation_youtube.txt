=== TITLE ===
How Cheap AI Models Are Made: Distillation Explained | Mini vs Flash vs Pro

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Under 30 minutes. Under ₹5,000 (under $50) of rented GPUs. That's what researchers say it took to make an existing open AI model beat OpenAI's o1-preview on competition maths. Its teacher? A smarter AI. This is distillation: how the cheap "mini" and "flash" models learn from the big ones.

In this video:
- The idea: a big, slow, expensive AI teaches a small, fast one
- Hidden lessons: an answer key just says "cat"; a teacher says 90% cat, 9% tiger, 1% car
- Chatbots do it with words: the student copies how the teacher weighs every next word
- Does it work? DistilBERT kept 97% of its teacher's score while being 40% smaller and 60% faster. Google said Gemini 1.5 Flash learned from 1.5 Pro this way
- The maths model: 1,000 worked solutions from a smarter AI, under 30 minutes, under ₹5,000
- The catch: students copy the teacher's mistakes, and big AI companies' terms ban training a rival on their answers
- Which one should you use? Emails, summaries, pulling out data: start with mini or flash. Multi-step reasoning, or it keeps slipping: switch to Pro. Test on 20 real examples first

Your turn: 500 office emails to summarise. Pro or Flash? Comment your pick.
