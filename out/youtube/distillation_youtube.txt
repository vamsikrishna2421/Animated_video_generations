=== TITLE ===
Why Flash and Mini AI Models Are So Cheap: Distillation Explained | Flash vs Pro

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Under 30 minutes. Under ₹5,000 (under $50) of rented GPUs. That's what researchers say it took to make an existing open AI model beat OpenAI's o1-preview (2024) on competition maths. Its teacher? A smarter AI. This is distillation: how the cheap "Flash" and "mini" models learn from the big ones.

In this video:
- The idea: a big, slow, expensive AI teaches a small, fast one
- Hidden lessons: an answer key just says "cat"; a teacher says 90% cat, 9% tiger, 1% car
- Chatbots do it with words: the student copies how the teacher weighs every next word
- Who uses it: in 2024, Google said Gemini 1.5 Flash learned from 1.5 Pro this way
- The maths model didn't copy percentages: it got 1,000 solved examples written by a smarter AI. The ₹5,000 paid only for that last step, on top of an already-trained open model
- The catch: students copy the teacher's mistakes, and big AI companies' terms ban training a rival on their answers
- Which one should you use? Emails, summaries, pulling out data: a small model (Flash, Flash-Lite, mini). Multi-step work, or it keeps getting it wrong: a bigger model like Gemini Pro (usually a paid plan). For developers paying per use, Pro costs about 3x Flash right now. Test 20 real examples first

Heads-up: from Oct 9, 2026, free Gemini app accounts get Flash-Lite only; Flash and Pro move to paid plans.
