=== TITLE ===
512 GB Laptop, "Not Enough Memory"? RAM vs Storage for Local AI (Tested) | AI From Scratch Ep 38 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Ravi anna buys a 512 GB laptop for his shop AI and it says "Not enough memory." 512 GB is storage, the godown that keeps the brain's file. To write each word the AI uses every number in its brain, so the whole brain has to sit in RAM, the counter: 8 GB on this laptop, and the full brain is 15 GB.

Our real test, internet cut, on a 4-core computer with no graphics card: the RAM each squeezed (4-bit) brain needed while answering. Qwen2.5 1.5B (1.1 GB file) 1.3 GB, 3B (2.1 GB file) 2.3 GB, 7B (4.7 GB file) 5.0 GB: the file plus a little room to work. With no internet the 7B still wrote a WhatsApp reply, in 17 seconds.

What it means for you: check your RAM, not your storage (Windows: Settings > System > About; Mac: About This Mac). RAM must hold the brain, its room to work and the computer itself: on 8 GB the 4.7 GB brain only just fits (close other apps); 16 GB has room for a 9 GB brain. Free apps, no coding: Ollama (type qwen2.5:7b) or LM Studio (search "qwen2.5 7b instruct", pick Q4_K_M). Download once, then it runs offline and your chats stay on your laptop. Want a bigger brain? More RAM, not a bigger disk.

How we tested (run by us): Qwen's own GGUF files, llama.cpp, context 4,096 tokens, each test in a network namespace with no internet, RAM measured as the peak memory with every weight loaded once.
Sources: Ollama library (qwen2.5 sizes) and FAQ; Ollama blog "Ollama's new app" (Jul 2025); LM Studio docs (offline operation, system requirements) and "free for work" (Jul 2025); Qwen2.5 model cards.
