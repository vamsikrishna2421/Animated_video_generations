=== TITLE ===
From 1 Upvote to 1,363: The Kerala Builder Behind Laya, the Free Open-Source Alternative to Jev

=== DESCRIPTION ===
In 2025, Nandakishor Mukkunnoth's post on Hacker News got 1 upvote: his own. In September 2026, three days after TypeSafe AI launched Jev with a $40M seed round, he released Laya, a free and open-source model for apps that need a quick yes, no or pick-one answer. This time the world noticed.

In this video:
- March 2025: his model that answers with a probability instead of writing text (arXiv 2503.23303). Almost nobody noticed.
- 15 Sep 2026: Jev launches. 18 Sep 2026: Laya, free and open source (Apache-2.0)
- He says he had the core idea first; critics, like Startup Fortune, say his 2025 model only handled sales
- Three weeks later: 1,363 upvotes on Hacker News, over 32,000 GitHub stars, about 317,000 downloads, and at least 10 research papers
- Who he is: CEO of Convai Innovations in Kasaragod, Kerala. In an interview with Analytics India Magazine, he said his family earned ₹200-300 a day in 2008 and that he got into a top science institute but couldn't afford it
- Before all this: a peer-reviewed paper on AI that reads ECGs (BMJ Digital Health & AI), six arXiv preprints in 2025, Hindi language models, and Nadhi Audit, an offline code-security app

Try Laya: pip install laya · github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya
