=== TITLE ===
40 Hours vs 40 Seconds: Who Owns an AI Poster? (Copyright & AI) | AI From Scratch Ep 46 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Meena drew her poster for 40 hours; Rohit's AI poster took 40 seconds and won. Who wins is the contest's rule; who owns it is the law's.

USA: an AI picture made with 624 prompts won a digital-art prize at the 2022 Colorado State Fair; the US Copyright Office refused to register it because the artist claimed the AI-made parts too. The Office says prompts alone don't make you the author; your own drawing and edits can be protected. India: the Copyright Act names "the person who causes the work to be created" for computer-generated work, untested on today's AI. India's DPIIT committee has proposed royalties for artists whose work trains AI (a proposal, not law).

Using AI in work you'll enter or sell: say you used it, add your own drawing, keep your drafts as proof.

Sources: US Copyright Office, Copyrightability report (Jan 2025); Thaler v. Perlmutter (D.C. Cir., Mar 2025); Copyright Office Review Board, Théâtre D'opéra Spatial (Sep 2023); India's Copyright Act 1957, Sec 2(d)(vi); DPIIT working paper (Dec 2025). Not legal advice.
