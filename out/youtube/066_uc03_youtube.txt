=== TITLE ===
₹1,450 a Bag on the Call, ₹14.50 in the AI Notes: How AI Meeting Notes Work | AI From Scratch UC03 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
AI meeting notes take three steps: ears (speech-to-text), who said what (names on the lines), then the notes. Each step passes its mistakes on. In our test (a made-up supplier call, a small free AI on our computer, 10 runs each), "fourteen fifty rupees a bag" became "14.50 rupees": 7 of 10 notes wrote ₹14.50 a bag and 5 of 10 totalled 40 bags at ₹580 (the real order: ₹58,000). Without names on who said what, 6 of 10 notes gave the shop owner's job to the supplier; with names, 10 of 10 right. Said in full ("one thousand four hundred and fifty rupees"), no note got the price wrong. Say amounts in full and check the notes before you forward them.
