=== TITLE ===
Deleted in 1 Tap, Kept 3 Years? What Happens to Your AI Chats (Privacy, Tested) | AI From Scratch Ep 44 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Amma pasted her bank statement, account number and PAN, into an AI chat to ask where her salary goes, then deleted it in one tap. Google's own Gemini privacy page: a subset of chats is read by human reviewers, and "reviewed chats are retained for up to three years", even after you delete your activity. It also says: "Please don't enter confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see."

Our real test (a small free AI, Qwen2.5 7B, offline on a 4-core computer, temperature 0.7, every reply read by hand): the same made-up October statement, 10 times with the IDs, 10 times blanked out. It named the right thing to cut (14 food orders) 8 of 10 times with the IDs and 10 of 10 blanked: it never needed them. (Its totals were wrong both ways: check an AI's maths.)

What it means for you: blank your name, account number, PAN and Aadhaar before you paste; a photo or PDF carries the numbers too; in Gemini turn off Keep Activity or use a temporary chat; in ChatGPT turn off "Improve the model for everyone".

Sources: Google, Gemini Apps Privacy Hub (updated Oct 26, 2026); OpenAI Help Center (Data Controls FAQ).
