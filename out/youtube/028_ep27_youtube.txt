=== TITLE ===
How AI Knows What "It" Means: Attention, the Key Idea Behind ChatGPT | AI From Scratch Ep 27

=== DESCRIPTION ===
"The animal didn't cross the street because it was too tired." You know "it" is the animal. Change "tired" to "wide" and "it" becomes the street. How does an AI work that out? Attention.

In this episode:
- Before Transformers: reading one word at a time, like a whisper down a line
- Attention: each word looks back at the earlier words and decides which ones matter
- Query, key, value as a question, a name tag and borrowed meaning
- Why long prompts cost more: double the prompt, roughly 4x the attention work
- 2017: "Attention Is All You Need" and the Transformer, the T in ChatGPT

Quiz: "The trophy didn't fit in the suitcase because it was too big." What is "it"? And if it was "too small"? Answer in the comments.
