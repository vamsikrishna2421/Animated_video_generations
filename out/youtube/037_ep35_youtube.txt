=== TITLE ===
The AI Brain That Didn't Fit: Small Language Models, Tested on a Shop's Messages | AI From Scratch Ep 35 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Ravi anna wants the shop's free AI brain inside his phone, so it works without internet. The phone's storage is full of good-morning photos. A model's size is its parameters, the numbers it's made of: 7 billion is a 4.7 GB file, 1.5 billion is 1.1 GB, half a billion is 0.5 GB. Brains small enough for a phone are called small language models.

Our real test, 40 shop WhatsApp messages sorted into order / payment / complaint / other: the tiny one got 14 right (random guessing gets about 10; it filed "Happy Diwali" as an order), the medium one 39, the big one 39, and the medium one was 4x faster than the big one. On 20 bills: 19, 9 and 2 mistakes.

How to use it: give each job a pass mark (sorting can live with one miss, a bill can't), try the sizes on your own examples, and keep the smallest that passes. For money, use a calculator.
Try one on your phone: free apps like PocketPal AI (open source) or Google's AI Edge Gallery download a small model once, then run it with no internet. Start with a 1B-size model.

How we tested (run by us): Qwen2.5-Instruct 0.5B, 1.5B and 7B, Qwen's own 4-bit GGUF files, llama.cpp on a laptop CPU, no graphics card. A small demo, not a benchmark.
Sources: Meta, Llama 3.2 (Sept 2024); Microsoft, Phi-3 technical report (Apr 2024); Apple's foundation-model reports (2024, 2025); Google Developers Blog, Gemma 3 on mobile (2025); Qwen2.5 model cards.
