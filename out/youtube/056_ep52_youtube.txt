=== TITLE ===
5 Years or ₹1,450? Build RAG Over Your PDFs, Part 1 | AI From Scratch Ep 52 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Our first PDF bot said the mixer's motor warranty was "Not in the document". Page 3 of the same PDF: covered for 5 years. It had read the cover page. Build it right in 3 steps: read the PDF page by page (pypdf); for a short PDF, don't search, send every page marked with its number; give it the rules (answer only from the document, say the page, or say it isn't there).

Our test, 10 questions on a 5-page warranty card we wrote: no PDF 0 of 10; the first search (it picked the cover 9 times) 1 of 10; all 5 pages, numbered, 9 of 10, with the right page on 7. The miss said "Not in the document" when page 4 had it. So open the page it gives, every time. (A small test on one small model, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, on our computer.)

Next: Part 2, PDFs too big to send whole.
