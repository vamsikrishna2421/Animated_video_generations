=== TITLE ===
How AI Turns Words Into Numbers: Tokens, Embeddings and the Next Token | AI From Scratch Ep 28

=== DESCRIPTION ===
To ChatGPT, "The Statue of Liberty is in" looks like 976 160801 328 51868 382 306. No words, only numbers. So how do those numbers turn into "New York"? This is Transformers, Part 2: following one sentence through the machine.

In this episode:
- Tokens: text is cut into words or pieces of words ("YouTube" = "You" + "Tube"), and each token gets an ID, just a label like a ticket number
- Embeddings: each ID fetches its row from a giant table, a long list of numbers that works like an address on a map of meaning. Google Maps needs 2 numbers; OpenAI's open model gpt-oss uses 2,880
- Word order: "dog" gets the same embedding first or last, so every token also carries its position
- Layers: on every floor, each word looks back and soaks up what matters (attention, from Episode 27). gpt-oss-120b has 36 layers
- The next token: score every possible next token, pick a likely one ("New"), climb again ("York"). Every new token is another climb, which is why answers appear piece by piece

Quiz: does "bank" get the same ID in "river bank" and "bank account"? Answer in the comments.
