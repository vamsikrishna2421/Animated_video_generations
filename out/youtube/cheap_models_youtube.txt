=== TITLE ===
Dirt-Cheap AI for Every Job (2026): $12 Instead of $1,200, and 4 Free Options

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Summarizing 100,000 customer emails costs about $1,200 on OpenAI's top model, and about $12 on its small sibling, GPT-6 Luna. For simple jobs, you rarely need the expensive model. Here are 5 jobs, 4 with a free option.

1. Questions, writing, homework: free ChatGPT or Gemini. $0. (Heads-up: free Gemini now runs on Flash-Lite, its smallest model.)
2. Free & private: Gemma 4 on your own laptop with the free Ollama app (Terminal: ollama run gemma4). 8 GB of RAM is enough. Private, not smarter.
3. Audio: Whisper on Groq (with a Q). Free plan: up to 8 audio-hours a day. Paid plan: 4¢ an hour. Voiceovers: Gemini text-to-speech, under 1¢ a minute.
4. Coding: GitHub Copilot Free (2,000 suggestions a month). Building an app? GPT-6 Luna: ~100 coding questions for ~10¢. Also cheap: DeepSeek Flash, $0.30 in / $1.20 out per 1M tokens (data may be stored in China).
5. Images: GPT Image 2, draft quality: $0.006 each. 100 drafts ≈ 60¢.

The rule: start cheap, test it on 20 real examples, move up only if it fails.

Which job do you use AI for most? Comment it and I'll reply with the cheapest pick.
