=== TITLE ===
₹168, With a Link: How AI Web Search Gets Fooled | AI From Scratch UC02 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
An AI with web search does three steps: search, read the top pages, answer with a link. The link shows where it read, not when. In our test (a made-up mini web of 6 pages, a small free AI on our computer, 10 fresh chats each), an old undated page titled "...price today" won 10 out of 10 times when the AI wasn't given today's date (₹168 instead of ₹142); given the date (by the app, or typed into the question), it found today's page 10 out of 10. Asking again without the date gave the same wrong price every time. The rule: open the link; "today" is not a date; no date? type today's date and ask again. Big apps slip differently: in a March 2025 Columbia Journalism Review (Tow Center) test, 8 AI search tools together got the source wrong in more than 60% of 1,600 questions.

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Web search unna AI moodu steps chestundi: vetakadam, paina pages chadavadam, link tho javaabu. Link ekkada chadivindo chupistundi, eppatido kaadu. Maa test lo (6 pages chinna web, maa computer lo chinna free AI, okkokati 10 kotha chats), eeroju date cheppakapothe date leni paata page 10 ki 10 saarlu gelichindi (₹142 badulu ₹168); date cheppithe (app gaani, prashna lo type chesi gaani) 10 ki 10 eeroju page. Date lekunda malli adigithe prati saari ade tappu dhara. Rule: link open chesi choodu; "today" ante date kaadu; date lekapothe eeroju date type chesi adugu. Pedda apps vere tappu: March 2025 Columbia Journalism Review (Tow Center) test lo 8 AI search tools kalipi 1,600 prashnallo 60% kanna ekkuva saarlu source tappu.
