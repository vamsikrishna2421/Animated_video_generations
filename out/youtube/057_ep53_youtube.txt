=== TITLE ===
15,373 Tokens or 247? Build RAG Over Your PDFs, Part 2 | AI From Scratch Ep 53 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
One question to a 300-page manual. The whole manual is 15,373 tokens and didn't fit our small AI; the 3 pages found by meaning are 247 tokens and answer it from page 141. Three steps: turn every page into numbers once (embeddings, all-MiniLM-L6-v2: 300 pages in 3.1 s on our computer); turn the question into numbers and take the 3 closest pages ("banging" found "knocking"); send those pages with their page numbers and Part 1's rules.

Our test, 10 questions on a manual we wrote for a made-up washing machine, right page in the top 3: by meaning 8 of 10, word search 7 of 10, both mixed the simple way 6 of 10. The 2 misses said "Not in the document"; when it says that, check the PDF yourself. Short PDF: send it whole (Part 1). Long, or lots of questions: search by meaning (Part 2), then test it with 10 questions whose page you know. (A small test on one small model, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, on our computer.)

Next: an agent with tools.
