=== TITLE ===
This Free AI Can't Write a Sentence. 32,000 People Starred It Anyway | Laya, Hands-On

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Laya, by Nandakishor from Kerala, is a free, open-source AI that never writes text. It answers a fixed question with one number: is this a refund request? Which team gets this ticket? How urgent is it? We ran it ourselves, then read the limits on his own page.

In this video:
- What it does: a refund email in, "YES, 94% sure" out (real output, base model, run by us)
- Three kinds of questions: pick one (which team: tech support, 95% sure), score (how urgent, 1 to 5: 2, 58% sure), yes/no (phishing: yes, 100% sure)
- Why people use it: about 33 ms a question on a cloud GPU (his measurement, on a T4), so 1,000 emails in about 33 seconds; 0.2 to 0.6 s a question on a 4-core CPU; free and open source (Apache-2.0); it can be wrong, but it can't ramble
- The catch, from his own page: great on what it was trained on (spam: 99% right). On new business tasks it gets 36% right out of the box, below the 46% you'd get by always picking the most common answer; 77% after training on that task's examples (his reported number). With many options, the paid rival Jev (by TypeSafe) does better: 87% vs 43% on a 77-option banking test (his page). Telugu: 22% on a 20-option test (English: 71%)
- Which one: English, a few options and examples to train on → Laya. Many options or no time to train → Jev (paid). Telugu → not yet
