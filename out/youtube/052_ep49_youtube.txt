=== TITLE ===
₹15,000 Helper vs a Free AI App: We Tested It on His Work | AI From Scratch Ep 49 #Shorts

=== DESCRIPTION ===
We gave a small free AI (Qwen2.5 7B, offline) the five paper tasks from a shop helper's day, five tries each: 19 of 25 right. Sums, GST and stock lists 15 of 15; every offer message had a blank or a slip to fix (our prompt never gave the shop's name). It can't carry sacks, deliver, or know the regulars.

AI takes tasks, not whole jobs: let it draft, give it the context, check before you send, and keep the work that needs hands and people. Worldwide, one job in four has tasks generative AI could do; most of those jobs are expected to change rather than vanish (ILO Working Paper 140, May 2025).
