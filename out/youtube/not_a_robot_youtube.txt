=== DESCRIPTION ===
Viral clips call the woman standing behind Trump in the Oval Office a robot. She's Susan Dell, co-founder and board chair of the Michael & Susan Dell Foundation; her husband founded Dell, the laptop company. The footage is from a real White House event on Oct 7, 2026. Here's how normal clips of her keep getting turned into "proof", and how to check the next one yourself.

The 4 tricks:
1. Slow it down. Slow motion makes normal blinks look mechanical. This isn't new: a slowed clip of her went viral in Feb 2026 too, and Lead Stories found the original C-SPAN video shows her moving normally.
2. Copies of copies, like a WhatsApp forward. One post with 4.5M views (as of Oct 11) is a phone filming a TV. Every copy smears detail.
3. AI "enhance" filters guess detail instead of recovering it; a Washington state court refused AI-enhanced video as evidence in 2024. In one viral 70-second compilation, close-ups of her face look filtered and warped (0:25, 0:54-0:56), while the frames just before look like normal TV footage. That's our own frame-by-frame check; we can't tell which tool was used.
4. Add a story: a red circle and a voice telling you what to see.

How to check the next one:
- Everyone moves like they're underwater → slowed
- Blur, screen glare, another app's logo → a copy
- Plastic-looking skin, eyes that change shape → a filter
- Mute it and ignore the circle; still weird? → find the full original
