Chinnu paid his phone money, ₹22,000, after a video of "the Finance Minister" promising ₹5,50,000 in a week. It was a deepfake.

Episode 45 of AI From Scratch: deepfakes and how to spot them
- A deepfake puts a real person's face and voice on words they never said; the AI learns them from the person's public videos
- PIB Fact Check (Apr 16, 2026): a viral video of the Finance Minister claiming ₹22,000 could return ₹5.5 lakh in a week is fake and made with AI. "Neither the Government nor the Finance Minister is endorsing any such investment scheme."
- RBI (Nov 2024) warned about deepfake videos of its Governor promoting investment schemes: "RBI does not give any such financial investment advice."
- The face test is weak: new fakes get the lips right. MIT Media Lab's Detect Fakes project: "there's no single tell-tale sign"

How to spot one, whoever's face is on it:
1. The money: quick, sure, huge returns = a scam
2. The source: the person's verified page, or PIB Fact Check. Not an ad, not a forward
3. Already paid? Call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in, the same day
