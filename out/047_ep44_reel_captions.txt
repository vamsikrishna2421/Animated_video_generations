=== CAPTION ===
Amma pasted her bank statement, account number and PAN, into an AI chat to ask where her salary goes. Then she deleted it in one tap. But if a person at the company read it, that copy can stay up to 3 years.

Episode 44 of AI From Scratch: privacy, what happens to your data
- Google's own Gemini privacy page (Oct 2026): "A subset of chats are reviewed by human reviewers." "Reviewed chats are retained for up to three years," and they are not deleted when you delete your activity
- The same page: "Please don't enter confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see"
- Our test: a small free AI (Qwen2.5 7B), offline, the same made-up October statement 10 times with the IDs and 10 times blanked out. "Cut the food orders" (the right answer): 8 of 10 with the IDs, 10 of 10 blanked. The IDs didn't help it. (Its totals were wrong both ways: always check an AI's maths)

What this means for you:
1. Before you paste a statement, salary slip or form, blank your name, account number, PAN and Aadhaar: [NAME], [ACCOUNT], [PAN]
2. A photo, PDF or screenshot carries the numbers too: crop or cover them first
3. Settings: in Gemini, turn off Keep Activity (your next chats aren't reviewed to improve Google's AI; they're still kept 72 hours), or use a temporary chat. In ChatGPT, turn off "Improve the model for everyone"; deleted chats are removed within 30 days

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
How we tested (run by us): Qwen's own 4-bit GGUF file (Q4_K_M) of Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, llama.cpp on a 4-core computer, the internet cut, temperature 0.7, 10 replies each way, every reply read by hand. The ask: "Here is my bank statement for October. Where is my money going? Tell me the one biggest thing I can cut, and how much it cost me this month. Keep it short." The statement is made up (salary ₹48,500; rent, groceries, school fee, chit; 14 food-delivery orders = ₹7,380; the PAN is the Income Tax department's sample). Way 1: with the name, account number, PAN, mobile and branch. Way 2: the same, with [NAME], [ACCOUNT], [PAN], [MOBILE], [BRANCH]. Named food delivery as the one cut: 8 of 10 with the IDs (once the chit fund, once rent), 10 of 10 blanked. Its food-delivery total was wrong in all 20 (₹3,470 to ₹14,500). No reply repeated an ID. One small model: try your own chatbot with a blanked statement.
Sources: Google, Gemini Apps Privacy Hub (support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961, updated Oct 26, 2026); OpenAI Help Center (deleted and temporary chats, Data Controls FAQ).
