=== CAPTION (English page) ===
Under 30 minutes. Under ₹5,000. That's what it took to make an existing AI beat an OpenAI reasoning model at maths, its researchers say. Its teacher? A smarter AI.

This is distillation: how cheap "mini" and "flash" models learn from big ones.
- The teacher doesn't just say "cat". It says 90% cat, 9% tiger, 1% car, and the student learns those hidden lessons
- Chatbots do the same with words: the student copies how the teacher weighs every next word
- DistilBERT kept 97% of its teacher's score while being 40% smaller and 60% faster
- Google said Gemini 1.5 Flash learned from 1.5 Pro this way
- The catch: students copy the teacher's mistakes, and big AI companies' terms ban training rivals on their answers

Which one should you use? Emails, summaries, pulling out data: start with mini/flash. Multi-step reasoning, or it keeps slipping: Pro. Test on 20 real examples first.
