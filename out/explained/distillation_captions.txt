=== CAPTION (English page) ===
Under 30 minutes. Under $50. That's what it took to make an existing AI beat OpenAI's o1-preview (2024) at competition math, its researchers say. Its teacher? A smarter AI.

This is distillation: how the cheap "Flash" and "mini" models learn from big ones.
- An answer key just says "cat". A teacher says 90% cat, 9% tiger, 1% car, and those small numbers are hidden lessons
- Chatbots do the same with words: the student copies how the teacher weighs every next word
- In 2024, Google said Gemini 1.5 Flash learned from 1.5 Pro this way
- That math model didn't copy percentages: it got 1,000 solved examples written by a smarter AI. The $50 paid only for that last step, on top of an already-trained open model
- The catch: students copy the teacher's mistakes, and big AI companies' terms ban training a rival on their answers

Which one should you use? Emails, summaries, pulling out data: a small model (Flash, Flash-Lite, mini). Multi-step work, or it keeps getting it wrong: a bigger model like Gemini Pro (usually a paid plan). For developers paying per use, Pro costs about 3x Flash right now. Test 20 real examples first.

Heads-up: from Oct 9, free Gemini app accounts get Flash-Lite only; Flash and Pro move to paid plans.
