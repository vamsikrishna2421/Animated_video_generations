=== CAPTION (English page) ===
This free AI from Kerala can't write a single sentence. Over 32,000 people starred it on GitHub anyway.

Laya (by Nandakishor, Kerala) doesn't write replies. It answers a fixed question with one number:
- Pick one: which team gets this ticket? (tech support, 95% sure)
- Score: how urgent, 1 to 5? (2, 58% sure)
- Yes or no: is this email phishing? (yes, 100% sure)
We ran these ourselves on the base model.

Why people use it:
- Fast: about 33 ms a question on a cloud GPU (his measurement, on a T4), so 1,000 emails in about 33 seconds; 0.2 to 0.6 s a question on a 4-core CPU
- Free and open source (Apache-2.0)
- It never writes, so it can be wrong, but it can't ramble

The catch, from his own page:
- Great at what it was trained on (spam: 99%). On new business tasks it scores 36% out of the box, below the 46% you'd get by always picking the most common answer. After training on that task's examples: 77% (his reported number).
- With 20+ answer options, the paid Jev (by TypeSafe) does better: 87% vs 43% on a 77-option banking test (his page).
- Telugu: 22% on a 20-option test (English: 71%).

So which one?
English, a few options, examples to train it on → Laya
Many options, or no time to train → Jev (paid)
Telugu → not yet
