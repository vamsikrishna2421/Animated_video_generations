=== CAPTION ===
Ravi anna found out he doesn't own his shop AI. He rents it.

Episode 34 of AI From Scratch: open vs closed AI models
- Closed model (the AI in most apps): its brain sits on the company's computers; you pay per question, and your orders go to them
- Open model: the company publishes the brain (the weights); you download it and run it on your own computer, even offline
- Our real test, 20 WhatsApp orders with known totals, internet off, a plain 4-core computer: free brains of 1 GB, 3 GB and 4.7 GB got 1, 9 and 18 bills right
- The 18/20 one still billed ₹566 for a ₹231 order, and took 15 seconds a bill
- Free to download is not free to run: the big open models run on a graphics card worth about ₹25 lakh

How to use this:
1. Rent (closed) when the job is hard and the data can leave your shop: it needs internet, and you pay per question
2. Own (open) when the data must stay home or the internet is patchy: free apps like Ollama and LM Studio run open models on a laptop
3. Either way, test it on 20 questions you already know the answers to (EP33), and keep a calculator for the adding

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
How we tested (run by us): 20 WhatsApp-style orders against a 15-item price board, totals worked out in advance. Three free open-weights models (Apache 2.0, from Hugging Face): Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (1.0 GB), Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (3.1 GB), and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct as Qwen's own 4-bit file (4.7 GB, run with llama.cpp, the way laptop apps run it). CPU only, 4 cores, no graphics card, nothing fetched from the internet during the test. Bills right: 1, 9 and 18 of 20, at about 1.4, 14 and 15 seconds a bill. The 7B's two misses: "3 biscuits, 1 tea powder, 1 kg sugar" billed ₹566 (right ₹231), and "1 kg each tomato onion potato rice" billed ₹177 (right ₹167).
Honest limits: 20 orders is a small demo, not a benchmark. We didn't test rented (closed) models, because they can't run offline on this computer. On public leaderboards the best closed models still lead, and the best open ones trail by a few months (Epoch AI, May 2026).
Open weights is not the same as open source: the Open Source Initiative's definition also asks for training-data information and code, and some "open" licenses add conditions (Llama: services with 700 million+ monthly users must ask Meta).
₹25 lakh: an 80 GB H100 is around $25,000 (Tom's Hardware, Feb 2025), and OpenAI says gpt-oss-120b runs on a single 80 GB GPU. Renting one in the cloud costs about $2.80 an hour (Tom's Hardware, Oct 2026).
