=== CAPTION ===
Ravi anna bought a 512 GB laptop for his shop AI. It said "Not enough memory."

Episode 38 of AI From Scratch: running AI on your own laptop
- Storage (the SSD or hard disk: 256 GB, 512 GB, 1 TB) is the godown: it keeps the brain's file
- RAM (8 GB, 16 GB) is the counter: to write each word, the AI uses every number in its brain (EP37), so the whole brain has to sit in RAM while it answers
- Our real test, internet cut (no network at all), on a 4-core cloud computer with no graphics card. RAM each squeezed (4-bit) brain needed while answering: Qwen2.5 1.5B (1.1 GB file) 1.3 GB · 3B (2.1 GB file) 2.3 GB · 7B (4.7 GB file) 5.0 GB. The file, plus a little room to work (more in long chats)
- With the internet cut, the 7B still wrote a WhatsApp reply, in 17 seconds

What this means for you:
1. Check your RAM, not your storage. Windows: Settings > System > About > Installed RAM. Mac: Apple menu > About This Mac
2. Your RAM must hold the brain, its room to work, and the computer itself (Windows, your browser). 8 GB: the 4.7 GB brain only just fits, close the other apps first (we measured the brain's 5.0 GB; we didn't run an 8 GB Windows laptop). 16 GB: room for a 9 GB brain such as Qwen2.5 14B squeezed (Ollama's file; we didn't run this one)
3. Free apps, no coding: Ollama or LM Studio. Download a brain once (that needs internet); after that it runs offline and your chats stay on your laptop. Ollama: type qwen2.5:7b. LM Studio: search "qwen2.5 7b instruct" and pick the Q4_K_M file
4. Slower than ChatGPT: 17 s for one reply on our computer (newer laptops are faster)
5. Want a bigger brain? More RAM, not a bigger disk (and check first whether your laptop's RAM can be upgraded: on many thin laptops it can't)

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
How we tested (run by us): Qwen's own 4-bit GGUF files (Q4_K_M) of Qwen2.5-1.5B, 3B and 7B Instruct, llama.cpp on a 4-core computer (server CPU, no graphics card), context 4,096 tokens (Ollama's default), every test in a network namespace with no internet (a lookup of huggingface.co and a connection to 1.1.1.1 both failed first). RAM needed = the most memory each brain held while writing a short reply, every weight loaded once: 1.34, 2.34 and 4.95 GB. The 7B's WhatsApp reply took 16.7 s, about 3 words a second.
"Why 5 and not 4.7?" The extra is room to work: the chat so far (the context) and working space. Longer chats need more.
"Private? Offline?" Ollama's FAQ: "Ollama runs locally. We don't see your prompts or data when you run locally." It also offers cloud models, and those use the internet. LM Studio's docs: "Nothing you enter into LM Studio when chatting with LLMs leaves your device." LM Studio is free at home and at work (since July 2025).
Licences: Qwen2.5 1.5B, 7B and 14B are Apache 2.0; the 3B is under Qwen's research licence (check it before business use).
