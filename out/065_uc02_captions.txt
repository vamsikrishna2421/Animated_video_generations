Use case 2 of AI From Scratch: how an AI answers with web search
- Three steps: it searches, reads the top pages, and answers with a link to where it read it.
- A link shows WHERE it read, not WHEN. An old page titled "...price in Hyderabad today", with no date on it, still says "today".
- Our test (a made-up mini web of 6 pages, a small free AI on our computer, 10 fresh chats each): not given today's date, it answered ₹168 from the old page 10 times out of 10. Given the date, it found today's page, ₹142, 10 out of 10.
- Asking again didn't help: 10 fresh chats, the same ₹168 every time.
- The big apps slip too, in a different way: in a March 2025 test by the Columbia Journalism Review's Tow Center, 8 AI search tools got the source of news quotes wrong in more than 60% of 1,600 tries (best 37% wrong, worst 94%).

Try it: ask your AI app for today's price of something you buy. Open its link: is there a date next to the number?

The rule:
1. Open the link
2. "Today" is not a date: find a real date next to the number
3. No date, no trust
Building an AI app? Put today's date in its instructions.
