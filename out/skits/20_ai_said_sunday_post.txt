=== CAPTION ===
The AI said Sunday. The calendar says Wednesday. 200 cards already printed.

A chat AI doesn't look at a calendar: asked the day of a date, it writes the day that sounds right, and a different one on different asks.
Our test: a small free AI on our laptop (offline, no tools), "What day of the week is 16 December 2026?" asked 10 times: Sunday 7, Tuesday 2, Wednesday once. 20 dates from Dec 2026 to Jun 2027, just the day: 6 right. Told to count from 1 January 2026: 2 right (it got lost in the sums). The date was fixed before the test, not picked after. Big apps are bigger and some can run a calculator: still, check the day.

For any date (functions, bookings, exams, EMI dues):
1. Open your phone's calendar. It counts; the AI guesses.
2. Building with AI? Work the date out in code and put it in the prompt.

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
How we tested: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (Qwen's own 4-bit file), llama.cpp on a 4-core computer, offline, no tools, temperature 0.7, a fresh chat each time, every reply read by hand. "What day of the week is 16 December 2026? Answer with just the day." 10 asks: Sunday 7, Tuesday 2, Wednesday 1. The same for 20 dates (16 Dec 2026 to 28 Jun 2027), once each: 6 right. With "Work it out step by step from 1 January 2026, which was a Thursday.": 2 right of 20 (12 ran out of room mid-sum without naming a day, 6 named a wrong day; one counted 2026 as 366 days). The family's date (16 Dec 2026) was written into the test before it ran. Vijaya atta, Mohan mama, Tarun and Sravya are made up; the news sting is our own.
