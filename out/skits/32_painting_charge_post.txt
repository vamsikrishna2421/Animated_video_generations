=== CAPTION ===
"I may have to move out after 4 months. What will it cost me?" The AI: "₹36,000", the lock-in penalty. It had all 14 clauses. Clause 11 also takes an ₹8,000 painting charge from the deposit. Real cost: ₹44,000. Of a ₹54,000 deposit, ₹10,000 comes back, not ₹18,000.

Our test: a sample rental agreement we wrote, a small free AI running offline on our computer, the same question 10 times. ₹36,000: 10 out of 10. The painting charge: 0 out of 10. We didn't test the big apps.

Then we asked: "List every amount of money I may have to pay or lose, and every condition on leaving early. Give the clause number for each." 10 out of 10 named the painting charge (clause 11).

So ask for every amount with its clause number, then open those clauses and add them up yourself. Before you send anything, black out names, address and ID numbers.
