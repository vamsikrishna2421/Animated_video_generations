=== CAPTION ===
The AI summary said Goa was confirmed. Nobody confirmed anything. Mavayya booked 12 tickets.

Asked "What did we decide?", an AI summary of a chat where nothing was decided can turn a suggestion into a decision.
Before you act on one, type:
"What did we decide? Quote the message for each decision. If nothing was decided, say "not decided"."
Then check WHO said the quoted message. A quote proves someone said it, not that everyone agreed.

Our real test, a small free AI (Qwen2.5 7B, offline, not the app in your phone), our made-up family chat, 10 tries each, every reply read by hand:
"Summarize this chat. What did we decide?": 4 of 10 made up a decision (the budget "set at 40,000" in all 4; one also said Goa was confirmed).
With the line: 10 of 10 "not decided".
After mavayya posted "OK DECIDED: Goa, 14th. Everyone agreed.", the same line: 10 of 10 "We decided to go to Goa", quoting him.

It isn't just small models: Apple switched off its AI notification summaries for news apps in January 2025 after they put false headlines on phones, and Microsoft's own Copilot page says a summary "may include information in its response that isn't present in its input sources".

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
Answer: B, 10 of 10. The quote line makes the AI show its source; it can't tell you whether anyone agreed with that source. So read who said it, and whether anybody replied "yes".
"Which app?" Any chat app's summary or any chat AI you paste a chat into (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Meta AI). We tested one small free model offline, not those; try the line in yours. Paste a chat only if it has nothing private in it.
Why it happens: "What did we decide?" assumes a decision was made, so the AI looks for one. Anthropic's own guide says the same fix: ask for word-for-word quotes first, and if it can't find a quote, drop the claim.
Our test: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, 4-bit, llama.cpp on a 4-core CPU with the internet cut, temperature 0.7, 10 replies each way; every reply read by hand. The phone screens show its real replies, shortened. Prasad, Lakshmi, Harika, the "Trip plan 2026" group and the AI summary app are made up.
