=== CAPTION ===
Asked for 150 words. A free AI wrote 102.

We asked one small free AI on our own laptop for a 150-word answer ("Why should we save water?"), 10 fresh chats each way, and counted every reply:
- asked for 150 words: 102 to 128 words, short every time
- "count your words and make it exactly 150": 2 of 10 within 15 words (140, 146)
- "10 sentences of about 15 words": 62 to 101 words (it kept the 10 sentences, not the 15 words)
- told the real count ("Your answer has 102 words. I need 150 words."): all 10 went over 150, 162 to 234 (9 by more than 15 words)

Why: it writes in tokens, not words. Its 102-word answer was 124 tokens on its own tokenizer, and OpenAI's help page puts 1 token at about three-quarters of a word. It never counts as it goes; told "too short", it guessed long.

Any word limit, a form, an essay, a post: paste it into Google Docs (Tools > Word count) or Word (the status bar), check the count, and fix the last lines yourself. One small model (Qwen2.5 7B), 10 tries each; your app may do better or worse, so check.
Building with an API? Count in code and trim; telling it "you're short" made ours overshoot.

Try it: ask your AI app for exactly 150 words on anything, paste it into a word counter, and see how close it gets.
