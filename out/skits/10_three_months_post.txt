=== CAPTION ===
The AI gave Thathayya 2 to 3 months. (It said his sugar test shows the PAST 2 to 3 months.)

Asking an AI about a report? Three words get you a textbook written for doctors. Before the doctor visit, type:
"Explain this to my 72-year-old grandfather in simple words. Then give 3 questions to ask his doctor."
Then paste the numbers from the report (hide the name). Change the who: Amma, me, a 10-year-old.

Our real test, a small free AI (Qwen2.5 7B, offline, not ChatGPT), 10 tries each way, every reply read by hand:
"HbA1c 7.2 meaning": doctor-talk ("glycated hemoglobin", ADA targets, "hypoglycemia") 10 of 10, about 190 words, no questions for the doctor.
With the line: doctor-talk 0 of 10, about 140 words ("like a report card for his diabetes"), and 3 questions for the doctor 10 of 10.

The AI explains. The doctor decides. OpenAI says its own ChatGPT Health "is not intended for diagnosis or treatment".
An Oxford study (Nature Medicine, Feb 2026, 1,298 people, 10 health cases): the AIs alone named the right condition 95% of the time; people using the same AIs, under 35%, no better than people without AI. Often they didn't tell it enough.

"Which app?" Any chat AI: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Meta AI. We tested one small free model offline, not those; try the line in yours. "Can I ask in Telugu?" Add "Reply in simple Telugu." (we tested English only). "Upload the PDF?" Many apps take a photo or a PDF; cover the name, phone number and ID first, or just type the numbers.
HbA1c is your average blood sugar over about the last 3 months (CDC: below 5.7% normal, 5.7-6.4% prediabetes, 6.5% or more diabetes). The right target for someone who already has diabetes depends on age and health: that's the doctor's call, which is why the line asks for questions, not a verdict.
Our test: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, 4-bit, llama.cpp on a 4-core CPU with the internet cut, temperature 0.7, 10 replies each way; every reply read by hand. All 10 three-word replies said "over the past 2 to 3 months"; two also had "a shorter life expectancy" (about treatment targets, not about him). The phone screens show its real replies, shortened. Its reply to "laddu sugar patient" asked what we meant, so the video cuts before it. Thathayya, Kamala, Sravani, Tommy and the "AI chat" app are made up.
The study: Bean et al., "Reliability of LLMs as medical assistants for the general public: a randomized preregistered study", Nature Medicine, Feb 9, 2026 (Oxford Internet Institute). GPT-4o, Llama 3 and Command R+; people were given symptom scenarios, not reports.
