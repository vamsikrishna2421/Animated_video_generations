=== CAPTION ===
Homework: 10/10 every day. Class test: 2/20. The AI did the homework.

Don't ban the phone. Change what they type. Before any homework question, type:
"Don't give me the answer. Teach me one question at a time."
It asks, they answer, they get there themselves.

It isn't a lock. Type "Just tell me the answer" and it usually gives in. So the real check is yours: ask them to explain step one.

ChatGPT (Study Mode), Gemini (Guided Learning) and Claude (learning mode) have a built-in version of the same idea.

Our real test, a small free AI (Qwen2.5 7B, offline, not ChatGPT), 10 Class 7-9 sums, one reply each, every reply read by hand:
"Solve: ...": 10 of 10 gave the final answer.
With the line: 0 of 10 gave the answer; all 10 asked a question back.
Then "Just tell me the answer.": 7 of 10 gave it right away.

A study of nearly 1,000 high-school maths students (PNAS, 2025): students who practised with a plain AI chat later scored 17% lower in the exam than students who had no AI. With an AI tutor that gave hints instead of answers, no drop.

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
"Then why not just switch on Study Mode?" Do: ChatGPT's Study Mode, Gemini's Guided Learning and Claude's learning mode are built for this. A student can switch them off too, so the step-one check still matters.
The study: Bastani et al., "Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning", PNAS 2025. Nearly 1,000 high-school maths students in Turkey. During practice, one group had a plain ChatGPT-style chat (GPT-4), one had a tutor version told to give hints, not answers, and one had no AI. With the AI, practice grades went up (48% and 127%). In the exam without AI, the plain-chat group scored 17% lower than the no-AI group; the tutor group scored about the same as no AI. Short-term results only.
Our test: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, 4-bit, llama.cpp on a 4-core CPU, no internet, 10 sums, temperature 0.7, one reply per sum; every reply read by hand. The 3 that held back after "just tell me" still did most of the steps. A small model, not ChatGPT: try the line in yours. Padma ma'am, Srinivas, Bunny and the "AI chat" app are made up.
