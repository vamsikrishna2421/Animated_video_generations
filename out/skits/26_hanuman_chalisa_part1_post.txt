=== CAPTION ===
Hanuman Chalisa, Part 1: see his form.

You know these words. Here is a picture for each line, to hold in your mind the next time you chant or hear it.

1. Jai Hanuman gyan gun sagar, jai Kapis tihun lok ujagar
Victory to Hanuman, ocean of wisdom and goodness; greatest of the vanar warriors, the light of the three worlds.
2. Ram doot atulit bal dhama, Anjani-putra Pavan-sut nama
Ram's messenger, home of matchless strength; son of Anjani, son of the Wind.
3. Mahabir bikram Bajrangi, kumati nivar sumati ke sangi
The great hero, with a body strong as a thunderbolt; he drives away bad thoughts and stays with good ones.
4. Kanchan baran biraj subesa, kanan kundal kunchit kesa
Golden in colour, in fine clothes; earrings in his ears, curly hair.

Part 2 next: chaupais 5-8.

Text: the Hanuman Chalisa by Goswami Tulsidas (16th century, in Awadhi); chaupais 1-4 come after the two opening dohas. Meanings checked against two translations that agree: Art of Living ("Hanuman Chalisa lyrics meaning", English) and Drik Panchang (Hindi). The drawing is ours, one design for every part: golden skin, curly hair under a gold crown, gold earrings, a saffron dhoti, the munj thread, the mace and the saffron flag. ########## TELUGU (@ai_maastaaru_telugu) ##########
=== CAPTION ===
Hanuman Chalisa, Part 1: aayana roopam.

Ee maatalu meeku nottiki vachchu. Prati line ki oka chitram: inkosaari Chalisa chadivetappudu, vinnappudu, manasulo darshinchukondi.

1. Jai Hanuman gyan gun sagar, jai Kapis tihun lok ujagar
Hanumantudiki jayam: gnaana guna saagarudu; vaanara shreshthudu, moodu lokaalaki velugu.
2. Ram doot atulit bal dhama, Anjani-putra Pavan-sut nama
Sri Ramuni doota, evariki lenanta balam; Anjani putrudu, Vaayu putrudu.
3. Mahabir bikram Bajrangi, kumati nivar sumati ke sangi
Mahaaveerudu, vajradeham; chedu aalochanalu tarimesi, manchi aalochanalaki toduga untaadu.
4. Kanchan baran biraj subesa, kanan kundal kunchit kesa
Bangaaru rangu, andamaina vastraalu; chevulaku kundalaalu, ungaraala mungurulu.

Part 2 tvaralo: chaupais 5-8.
