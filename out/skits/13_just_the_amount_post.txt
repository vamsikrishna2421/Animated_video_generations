=== CAPTION ===
An AI said ₹1,059 each. The calculator said ₹822.07.

Asked for "just the amount", a small AI doesn't work the sum out. It writes a guess.
Our test: a tiny free AI (Qwen2.5 3B, offline on a 4-core laptop; not ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude), the same bill 10 times:
"Reply with just the amount each person pays": 0 of 10 right (₹220 to ₹1,139), about 1 second a reply.
"Show the working step by step": 9 of 10 right, about 50 seconds a reply.
Big apps usually do better at sums; we didn't test them. The habit works on any app:
1. For money, type "Show the working step by step."
2. Check each line on your calculator. A wrong step shows up in the working; a wrong final number on its own doesn't.

The bill: food ₹2,847 + 5% GST, then a 10% tip on the total, split 4 ways = ₹822.07 each.
Researchers see it too: Apple's GSM-Symbolic study (Oct 2024) found "the performance of all models declines when only the numerical values in the question are altered". Google's own Gemini help page: "Gemini can hallucinate and present inaccurate information as factual."

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
Every result (10 replies each way, every reply read by hand; right = within ₹1 of ₹822.07):
Qwen2.5-3B: just the amount 0 of 10 (₹220.23, ₹965.63, ₹241.25, ₹1,059, ₹220.23, ₹1,068.25, ₹1,028.64, ₹1,138.88, ₹849, ₹886.50) · show the working 9 of 10 (the 10th had the right total and hit our 400-token limit before dividing by 4) · "write it as one calculator expression" 6 of 10 (its misses were wrong formulas: × 4 where it says ÷ 4, the tip on the wrong amount).
Qwen2.5-1.5B, the smaller one: just the amount 6 of 10, because it ignored "just the amount" and worked it out anyway (4 slips, e.g. 2,847 × 1.05 written as 2,993.35) · show the working 6 of 10 (3 hit the limit, 1 slip: 2,847 + 142.35 written as 3,089.35, the kind of line a calculator check catches) · one expression 5 of 10.
So what helped was the working, not the bigger model. The working is also slower: about 50 s instead of 1 s on our laptop.
How we tested: Qwen's own 4-bit files, llama.cpp, 4 CPU cores, the internet cut, temperature 0.7, up to 400 tokens a reply; the test script was committed before it ran. Arjun, Sneha, Nikhil, Meghana and the biryani place are made up.
