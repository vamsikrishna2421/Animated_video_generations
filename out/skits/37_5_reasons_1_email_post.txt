=== CAPTION ===
5 people. 5 different reasons. 1 AI-written leave email: "...due to personal reasons."

We typed the usual vague ask into a small free AI, 10 fresh chats: "Write a leave email to my manager for 2 days, Thursday and Friday, for a personal reason." All 10 opened "I hope this message (or email) finds you well". None gave a real reason. 4 made up dates we never gave it.

Then we gave it the real facts: a sister's engagement in Vijayawada, back Monday, the report done, a colleague covering the support tickets. The facts came through. But all 10 guessed a month, 3 handed the colleague the wrong job, and 1 signed off with the AI's own name.

Try it before your next leave email: type the vague ask into the AI app you use and read the first line. Then give it 3 things: the reason you're comfortable sharing, your dates and return day, who covers your work. Before you send: every [bracket], every date, every name.

Ramya, Kiran and Sravya are made up. The emails and counts are from our test.
