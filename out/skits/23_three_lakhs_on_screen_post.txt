=== CAPTION ===
The app says he has ₹3,11,593. He can take out ₹0. It wants an 18% "tax" first.

"AI trading bot" is a costume. No real trading makes a profit every single day: real markets fall some days. A balance that rises exactly 3% every day for 15 days is ₹2,00,000 × 1.03, fifteen times: ₹3,11,593, to the rupee. That's a calculator painted green. If it were real, ₹2 lakh would be about ₹970 crore in a year.

Two signs of a scam, AI or not:
1. The same exact % profit every single day, no bad days
2. A "tax" or fee you must pay before you can withdraw (where tax applies, a real platform cuts it from your money or you file it yourself; none asks you to pay first to unlock your money)

Already paid? Don't pay more. Call 1930 now (or cybercrime.gov.in): the sooner you report, the better the chance the money can still be held. Before you invest: check the name on SEBI's list of registered intermediaries (sebi.gov.in). Fake apps borrow real firms' names, so check the app's own details too.
