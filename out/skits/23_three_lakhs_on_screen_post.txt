=== CAPTION ===
The app says he has ₹3,11,593. He can take out ₹0. It wants an 18% "tax" first.

"AI trading bot" is a costume. No real trading makes a profit every single day: real markets fall some days. A balance that rises exactly 3% every day for 15 days is ₹2,00,000 × 1.03, fifteen times: ₹3,11,593, to the rupee. That's a calculator painted green. If it were real, ₹2 lakh would be about ₹970 crore in a year.

Two signs of a scam, AI or not:
1. Profit every single day, no bad days
2. A "tax" or fee before you can withdraw (real tax goes to the government, in your tax return, not to the app)

Already paid? Don't pay more. Call 1930 now (or cybercrime.gov.in): the sooner you report, the better the chance the money can be held. Before you invest: check the name on SEBI's list of registered intermediaries (sebi.gov.in). Fake apps borrow real firms' names, so check the app's own details too.

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
The maths: ₹2,00,000 × 1.03^15 = ₹3,11,593 (the app's balance on day 15, to the rupee); 18% of it = ₹56,087; ₹2,00,000 × 1.03^365 ≈ ₹969.7 crore. SEBI: "no guarantees of assured returns in the securities market" (investor caution, Feb 2024, as reported by Business Standard and Moneylife); its June 2022 caution on unregulated platforms selling algo strategies with claims of huge returns (PR 20/2022). The pattern in 2026 police cases: WhatsApp/Telegram groups, a fake trading app with a growing dashboard, a small early withdrawal for trust, then a "tax" or fee before any big withdrawal (Free Press Journal: Mumbai, Indore; Etemaad Daily: Hyderabad). 1930 is the national cyber-fraud helpline (I4C, Ministry of Home Affairs).
