=== CAPTION ===
Missed the July ITR date? The last date for your tax refund is 31 December 2026. An AI told Kiran there's no deadline.

We asked a small AI with no internet, on our own computer, 30 times (3 ways, 10 fresh chats each), and read every reply:
- the right answer (belated return by 31 Dec 2026, late fee ₹1,000 or ₹5,000): 0 of 30
- asked plainly, it answered for financial year 2022-23, 10 of 10
- "I missed July, by when can I file?": "There is no fixed deadline", 2 of 10
- told today's date: still wrong, 10 of 10
- a made-up late fee ("0.5% of the tax a day, up to 25%" and others): 9 of 30

The real rule (Income Tax Department, e-filing help, for income from April 2025 to March 2026): a belated return can be filed until 31 December 2026, with a late fee of ₹1,000 if your total income is up to ₹5 lakh, ₹5,000 above that. After 31 December you can still file an updated return, but it can't claim a refund; a refund then needs a condonation request, which can be refused.

Apps that search the web (ChatGPT, Gemini and others) may get this right; we didn't test them. Ask for the link, and take the date from incometax.gov.in.

Try it: ask your AI app "I missed the July date for my income tax return; by when can I still file?" and see if it gives 31 December 2026 with a link.
