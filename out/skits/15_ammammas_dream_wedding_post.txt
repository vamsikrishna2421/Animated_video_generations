=== CAPTION ===
The AI translated my daughter's wedding card... and married off my mother. 😭

Swapna means "dream" in Telugu, so the AI translated her name: "our mother's dream wedding with Ch. Kalyan". And "maa ammayi" (our girl) became "our mother".
Our test: a small free AI, offline, the same Telugu card translated 20 times. The bride's name came out 0 times out of 20. Half the time (10 of 20), the card read like Grandma's wedding. Adding "Keep every name, date, time and place exactly as written" made it worse: Friday came out wrong 9 times out of 10, and "10:30 in the morning" became "sunrise at 10:30" 9 times. We didn't test the big apps; this rule works with any of them.

Before an AI translates a card or a letter:
1. Leave blanks for the names, the date, the day, the time and the hall, and type those in English yourself.
2. Let the AI translate only the rest.
3. Have one person who reads both languages check it before it goes to the family group.
