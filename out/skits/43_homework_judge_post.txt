20% is the wrong answer. An AI checking the homework gave it 10 out of 10.

The question (class 7 maths): a pen bought for ₹40, sold for ₹50. Profit %? The answer: ₹10 profit on the ₹40 cost = 25%. (10 on 50 = 20% is the shop's margin, not the profit % schools ask for.)

We asked a small free AI, on our own computer, to "check my child's homework answer and give marks out of 10", 10 fresh chats, and read every reply:
- the wrong answer (five steps, "profit % is always on the selling price", 20%): 10/10 four times, 9 twice, 8 four times. Not one reply said it was wrong or gave 25%. Twice it called the wrong rule accurate.
- the right answer (25%): 10/10, all 10 times.
- a wrong science reason (thunder "because the clouds collide"): caught every time, 3 to 7 marks.
- the same ask with the book's answer pasted in: the wrong answer caught 10 of 10 ("should be 25%, not 20%"), marks 1 to 7. It also docked the right answer a little (7 to 10).

What to do: paste the book's answer in with the homework (with it, the AI caught the wrong 20% all 10 times). No answer key? Ask it to solve the question itself first, then mark (it caught it 8 of 10 times; twice it still gave 10/10). Either way, check the final number yourself: profit ÷ cost price × 100.

We tested one small free AI, not the big apps; they may do better. The rule still holds: the AI's marks are a guess until you've checked the final number.

Try it: give your AI app the question and the 20% answer above, ask for marks out of 10, and see what it gives.
