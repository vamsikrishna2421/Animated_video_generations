########## ENGLISH (@ai_maastaaru) ##########
=== CAPTION ===
Policy: claim within 30 days. The AI's Telugu: 30 weeks. Same number, different unit, and a claim you can lose.

We gave a small free AI on our computer a 10-line health policy we wrote and asked: "Translate this into Telugu for my mother." 5 fresh tries. "30 days" came out right once; once it said 30 weeks. "Covered only after 2 years" once became 2 weeks. "Maternity: not covered" never survived; one reply said birth expenses "will be settled".

Asking it to keep every number and every "not" fixed "30 days" (4 of 5), but "2 years" was still right only 2 of 5, and "not covered" came through 0 of 5. The digits look right, so a quick glance passes it.

Try it this week: ask your agent, customer care or your insurer's app for your policy's Customer Information Sheet in Telugu (IRDAI's rule since Sep 2024: insurers must give it on request). It is a summary; the policy wording stays the reference. If you use any app to translate a money paper, check every day, week and month, and every "not", with someone who reads both languages.

Sarojini garu is made up. The test is real. ChatGPT, Gemini and Google Translate were not tested.
