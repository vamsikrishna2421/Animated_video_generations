=== CAPTION ===
Job open for 8 months. 0 hires. The list said ".NET". Every resume said "dotnet".

A keyword filter compares letters, not meaning: ".NET" and "dotnet" are different strings to it, though Microsoft's own .NET command-line tool is literally called dotnet.
Our test: 20 ways developers really write .NET, through an exact ".NET" filter: 11 bounced (dotnet, DotNet, DOTNET, .Net, dot net, NET 6, ASP.Net...). And of 5 lines with no .NET skill, it let 3 in, including "Not familiar with .NET".
A small free AI that reads meaning did better: nobody without .NET got in. But it still bounced real ones 13 times out of 60 (about 1 in 5), "DotNet" as one word 3 times out of 3.

Job hunting: copy the job post's exact words, letter for letter (it says .NET? write .NET; it says B.Com? write B.Com).
Hiring: read 20 rejects yourself every week, and add every spelling to the list.

(A made-up company, inspired by a story going around LinkedIn. Our test is real.)
