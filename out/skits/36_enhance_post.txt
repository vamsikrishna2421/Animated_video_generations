########## ENGLISH (@ai_maastaaru) ##########
=== CAPTION ===
Same blurry CCTV frame. AI enhance, try 1: 9000. Try 2: 9010. The real plate: 5660.

"Enhance" tools built on generative AI don't read a blur; they draw a sharp picture that looks likely. We tested one: a free AI upscaler on our computer, 10 blurry number plates, 3 tries each. It drew 20 sharp-looking plates; the last 4 digits were right once. Even on clearer photos, 9 of 30 came back with a character changed.

A US court said the same in 2024: it barred AI-enhanced video from a murder trial, because it showed things that weren't in the original (NBC News).

Try it: photograph a number plate from far away, run it through an AI upscaler or your photo app's "enhance", and compare it with the real plate. Same letters? Next time something happens in your building: a second camera, the watchman, the register. Never blame anyone from an enhanced picture.

Vijaya garu and Mohan garu are made up. The plates and the AI outputs are from our test.

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
How we tested (Nov 21, 2026): 10 made-up plates in the Telangana format, drawn sharp, shrunk to a CCTV-sized blur (36x9 and 52x13 pixels of plate), then enlarged 4x by the Stable Diffusion x4 upscaler (an open generative model) on our computer, 20 steps, 3 seeds each; every output read by hand. Far blur: 20 sharp-looking plates, last 4 digits right in 1; one seed gave streaks all 10 times. Clearer blur: 21 of 30 right, 9 with a character changed (9233 → 9Z33, 5822 → 5S22). Phone and photo-app enhance tools were not tested. Code: pipeline/creative36/enhance_demo.py.
