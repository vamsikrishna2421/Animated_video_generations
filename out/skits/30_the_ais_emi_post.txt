₹23,405 or ₹34,713? The same ₹40 lakh home loan (8.5%, 20 years). The first came from a small free AI with no calculator. The second is the real EMI.

Plan your budget on the first, and you're ₹11,308 short every month: ₹27 lakh over 20 years.

Asked to work it out step by step, it got closer (₹33,092) and even wrote: "This value is calculated using a calculator or financial software." It had no calculator. A chat AI writes the next likely words; with numbers, it guesses the hard step.

Our test, 10 loans (bike, car, home, education...), right to the rupee:
- asked plainly: 0 of 10 (off by ₹2 to ₹22,137 a month)
- step by step: 0 of 10 (closer: off by ₹32 to ₹1,621 a month)
- asked for the formula, which we then worked out: 4 of 10 exact; the other 6 formulas were far off
A small free AI on our computer with no tools. Big apps can run real code for maths (OpenAI's and Google's own pages say so): ask them to, then match the answer with your bank's EMI calculator.

For money maths: a calculator, not a chat.
