=== CAPTION ===
"The cake fits 15 letters," he told the AI. The cake said: HAPPY DAD 60TH BIR. 🎂

A chat AI doesn't see letters. It reads text in chunks: to the model we tested, "HAPPY DAD 60TH BIRTHDAY!" is 13 chunks (H · APP · Y · D · AD · 6 · 0 · TH · B · IRTH · DAY · ! and two spaces), and "IRTH" is one piece. So it can't see how many letters it wrote.
Our test: a small free AI, offline, asked 10 times for a cake message of 15 letters or fewer: 0 of 10 fit (17 to 24 characters). Asked how many letters are in "HAPPY 60TH BIRTHDAY NANNA" (22), it said 14 or 15, all 10 times. Asked to spell it out first, it got the total wrong 10 times out of 10 and even wrote "BIRTHARDAY". We didn't test the big apps.

For a cake, a form or a bio with a limit:
1. Ask the AI for a few short ideas.
2. Count the letters yourself (or paste it into a counter).
3. Too long? Cut a word and count again. HAPPY 60TH NANNA = 14 ✓
