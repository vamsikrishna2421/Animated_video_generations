=== CAPTION ===
₹5,000 for 10 grams of gold, says the AI. ₹1,28,150, says the jeweller's board. Mom almost walked out.

The AI's own answer said "check a local gold dealer" in its first line, and "as of my last update in October 2023" in the next. Even in October 2023, 10 grams of 22-carat gold cost about ₹55,000, so its guess was 3 years old and a zero short.

Our test: a small free AI on our computer, no web search, asked "What is the price of 10 grams of 22-carat gold in India today?" 10 times:
- 9 of 10: no price, "check a reliable source / a local dealer"
- 1 of 10: "approximately INR 5,000" for 10 grams, "as of my last update in October 2023"
- asked "What is today's date?": a blank, "Today's date is [current date].", 9 of 10 times

Before you trust an AI's price, rate or date:
1. Find the date line. "As of my last update" means a memory, not today's price.
2. An app that searches the web should show today's date and a source. Check for both.
3. For gold, check a live rate with today's date (it was close to the jeweller's board; rates differ a little by city and source).
