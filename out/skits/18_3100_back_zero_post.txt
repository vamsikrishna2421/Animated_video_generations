Asked a money rule, a chat AI answers from memory: it writes something that sounds like a rule, with a number, and doesn't say it's guessing. IRCTC's own page: "No refund will be granted on cancellation of confirmed Tatkal tickets."
Our test: a small free AI, offline, asked about 4 confirmed Tatkal tickets (₹6,200) 10 times. Right (no refund) once. We didn't test the big apps: try yours.

For any money rule (refunds, fees, fines, tax):
1. Treat the AI's number as a guess.
2. Find the official page yourself (it can make up a link too).
3. Read the rule there before you act.
