=== CAPTION ===
₹2,000 per family, for a flood video made in 4.5 minutes. We made this fake ourselves with a free AI on our own computer (no graphics card), then ran 3 checks on it.

1. PAUSE IT. Stop on a sign, a hand, a face, an umbrella. We asked for a sign that says AMEERPET: in 48 of 48 frames it is not a word. In one shot, one umbrella splits into two in under half a second (frame 4 to frame 7). At full speed on a phone, you won't see it.
2. LOOK FOR A LABEL ("AI info" on Instagram, "Altered or synthetic content" / "How this content was made" on YouTube). Apps add it when the maker says so, when the file carries a tag like C2PA Content Credentials, or when their own systems catch it. Our file carried no tag at all, so no label proves nothing.
3. ASK BEFORE YOU FORWARD OR PAY: send it to PIB Fact Check on WhatsApp, +91 87997 11259 (factcheck.pib.gov.in). Never pay a UPI ID from a forward; give to relief only through an official fund.

Honest limits: paid video AI makes much cleaner fakes, with fewer clues to pause on, so do all three. We tested our own fake only (no real clip, no detector apps).

Try it today: open the last flood, accident or "breaking" video in your family group, pause it on a sign or a hand, and check under it for a label before anyone forwards it again.

How we made and tested the fake (Nov 19, 2026): AnimateDiff-Lightning (ByteDance, 4-step, CreativeML OpenRAIL-M licence) on the epiCRealism base (Stable Diffusion 1.5, CreativeML OpenRAIL-M), on a 4-core CPU with no graphics card: 3 shots x 16 frames (432 x 768, 8 frames a second = 6 s) in 268 s. Every shot it made is shown; nothing re-rolled. Our file scan found no C2PA Content Credentials and no IPTC AI tag in the metadata. When apps label AI: YouTube Help, "Disclosing use of altered or synthetic content" (creator disclosure, C2PA metadata, YouTube's own AI tools, or YouTube's systems); Meta's newsroom post on labelling AI content (updated July 1 and Sept 12, 2024: "AI info" when it detects industry-standard AI indicators or when people disclose). PIB Fact Check: factcheck.pib.gov.in, WhatsApp +91-8799711259. Script and results: pipeline/howto05/fake_demo.py.
