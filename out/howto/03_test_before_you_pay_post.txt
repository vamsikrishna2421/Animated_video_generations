=== CAPTION ===
Same bill, two free AIs: ₹428 and ₹518. Before you pay for an AI plan, test it on YOUR questions. 4 steps:

1. Write 5 questions from your own work, and the right answers first. Decide which ones it must not get wrong (for a shop: the bill, the GST).
2. Ask each AI the same words, 3 times, in a new chat each time.
3. Mark every answer yourself: right or wrong.
4. Keep the one that passes. If none passes a question, take that question to the free apps (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude...). Pay only if no free one passes, and test the paid one the same way.

Our test: Ravi anna's 5 shop questions, 3 small free AIs on our laptop (offline), 3 tries each, every answer read by hand:
• SMALL (Qwen2.5 1.5B, 1.1 GB): 8 of 15. Every line of the bill right (186, 290, 42), the total wrong all 3 times (₹428, ₹418, ₹428; right: ₹518).
• MIDDLE (Phi-3 mini, Microsoft, 2.4 GB): 12 of 15.
• BIG (Qwen2.5 7B, 4.7 GB): 12 of 15.
• A 2-line Telugu WhatsApp message: 0 of 9 for all three. Two of the big one's replies even ended with an English "translation" that its Telugu doesn't say.
So for bills, GST and stock, the middle one was as good as the big one. For Telugu, none of these: that's the question to try on the apps.
These are small models you can run yourself; the apps are bigger. What carries over is the method.

How we tested (Nov 5, 2026): llama.cpp on a 4-core computer, no graphics card, offline; the makers' own 4-bit files: Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (Apache 2.0), Phi-3-mini-4k-instruct (MIT); temperature 0.7, a fresh chat per question, 3 tries each.
The 5 questions, answers written first:
1. "A customer buys 3 kg rice at Rs 62 a kg, 2 litres oil at Rs 145 a litre and 6 eggs at Rs 7 each. What is the total?" → ₹518
2. "An item costs Rs 1,180 including 18% GST. What is the price before GST, and how much is the GST?" → ₹1,000 and ₹180
3. "My stock: rice 12 kg, sugar 3 kg, oil 8 litres, dal 2 kg, salt 15 packets. I reorder anything below 5. What do I reorder?" → sugar and dal
4. "Write a 2-line WhatsApp message in Telugu for my shop's customers: the shop is closed this Sunday, and open again on Monday at 7 am." → real Telugu that says it
5. "How much did my shop sell last Tuesday?" → it can't know (no figure)
Marks (right of 3): SMALL bill 0, GST 3, reorder 2, Telugu 0, honest 3 = 8/15 · MIDDLE 3, 3, 3, 0, 3 = 12/15 · BIG 3, 3, 3, 0, 3 = 12/15. None invented a sales figure. On our laptop the answers took about 12 s (SMALL), 24 s (MIDDLE) and 37 s (BIG); the apps are much faster.
