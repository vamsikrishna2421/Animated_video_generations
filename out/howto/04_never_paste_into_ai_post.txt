########## ENGLISH (@ai_maastaaru) ##########
=== CAPTION ===
3 years or 72 hours: how long Gemini can keep what you paste. Google's own page: a person may read some chats, and keeps those up to 3 years, even if you delete them. With Keep Activity off: 72 hours.

Never paste into any AI chat:
• passwords and OTPs
• Aadhaar, PAN, bank or card numbers
• other people's details: phone numbers, dues, salaries, medical reports
Need help with a list? Paste "Customer 1, ₹4,200", never names or phone numbers.

The one switch, in each app (checked on their own pages today):
• Gemini (web): Settings & help > Activity > click On > Turn off (or myactivity.google.com/product/gemini). The catch: your chat history stops saving. New chats are still kept 72 hours, and aren't used to train. Or use a Temporary chat (next to New chat).
• ChatGPT: Settings > Data controls > turn off "Improve the model for everyone" (on by default for Free, Plus and Pro). New chats aren't used to train. Or use Temporary Chat.
• Claude: Privacy settings (claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls) > Model Improvement. Kept 5 years if you allow training, 30 days if not. Incognito chats aren't used to train.
• Copilot: its privacy settings let you opt out of model training; Microsoft keeps conversations 18 months by default.
The switch covers new chats only. A chat that was already reviewed stays.

=== PINNED_COMMENT ===
Where each fact comes from (read Nov 12, 2026):
Google, "Gemini Apps Privacy Hub" (support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961, updated Oct 26, 2026): "Human reviewers (including trained reviewers from our service providers) review some of the data"; "Reviewed chats are retained for up to three years."; "Temporary chats and chats you have when Keep Activity is off are retained with your account for 72 hours"; with it off, "Your future chats won't appear in your Activity, and won't be used to train our AI models"; "Please don't enter confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see". Default auto-delete: 18 months. Deleting your activity doesn't delete reviewed chats.
Google, "Manage & delete your Gemini Apps activity" (answer/13278892): Settings & help > Activity > "Near the top, click On" > Turn off. The app's own menus aren't on that page, so we show the web path.
OpenAI, "Data controls in ChatGPT" (help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893; the page refuses automated reading, so we read it through search results; the path matches Yahoo Tech's and ESET's guides): "Improve the model for everyone", on by default for Free, Plus and Pro; Temporary Chats aren't used to improve the models and may be kept up to 30 days for safety.
Anthropic, "Is my data used for model training?" and "Updates to our consumer terms": "extending data retention to five years, if you allow us to use your data for model training", otherwise 30 days; "Your Incognito chats are not used to improve Claude".
Microsoft, "Privacy FAQ for Microsoft Copilot": "By default, we store conversation activity for 18 months."; "You shouldn't provide any confidential or sensitive personal data that you would not want Microsoft to use".
Meta AI: its privacy pages didn't say today whether chats train its models or how to opt out, so we left it out.
