# Template idea: "text wall" opener (from NYT Games reel Dd9GeOtAArn, 15 s, watched via vidIQ)

- 0-5 s: grey wall of a repeated bold line ("FOCUS ON FUN.") in staggered rows sliding sideways; a toggle flips
  ("ADS OFF"), then rapid vertical wipes through flat colour blocks (orange / pink / blue), cuts every ~1 s.
- 5-8 s: solid yellow wipes up; one bold black headline, centred, static.
- 8-15 s: price line + black CTA button + small terms.
- Audio: a click on frame 1, then clap/tap percussion; no voice. Cuts land on claps.

Our version: rows of a repeating hook phrase ("AI IS CHEAPER THAN YOU THINK.") sliding in alternate directions,
one row flips colour on the first beat, then wipe into the explainer. Click + clap track from our own synth.
