# SYSTEM 1 vs 2
HOOK: Two plus two? You answered instantly. Seventeen times twenty-four? You had to stop and think. AI just split the same way. Here's System 1 versus System 2.
[THE IDEA · Fast brain, slow brain] Psychologist Daniel Kahneman named them. System 1 is fast and automatic, like two plus two. System 2 is slow and deliberate, like working out seventeen times twenty-four step by step.
  visual: fumble — SYSTEM 2 · THINKING
[SYSTEM 1 AI · AI that just decides] Decision models like TypeSafe's Jev are System 1 AI. You give them a question and a fixed list of answers, like spam or not spam. They don't write a word. They pick one, with a confidence, like spam, 97 percent, in under half a second.
  on screen: QUESTION: Is this email spam? | ANSWER: spam · 97% sure | SPEED: 70–500 ms (TypeSafe's claim)
  visual: route — SYSTEM 1 · ONE QUICK DECISION
[SYSTEM 2 AI · AI that thinks first] Reasoning models, like ChatGPT's thinking mode or OpenAI's o-series, work like System 2. They reason step by step, so they handle math, code and planning, but they take seconds. TypeSafe says those models take 3 seconds or more, where Jev answers in under half a second.
  visual: bars — TIME TO ANSWER · LOWER IS FASTER
[USE BOTH · Fast first, think when needed] The smart setup uses both. Spam check? System 1. Route a ticket? System 1. Next game move? System 1. Write a reply, solve math, plan a trip? Send those to System 2.
