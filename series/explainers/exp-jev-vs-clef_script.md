# JEV vs CLEF
HOOK: Two System 1 models. One is cheaper. One is faster. Neither wins everything. Here's Jev versus Clef-flash.
[MEET THEM · Closed vs open] Jev is TypeSafe's closed model. You can only use it through their service, and it reads text only. Clef-flash is Cloudflare's open model. Anyone can download it, and it can also read images. Both answer the same kind of questions the same way.
  on screen: JEV: closed · text only | CLEF-FLASH: open · free download · reads images | BOTH: same question format
[PRICE · Jev is cheaper] Say you sort 10,000 emails. On Jev that costs about 13 cents. On Clef-flash, about 27 cents. Jev only answers with a label, so it charges nothing for the answer. Clef-flash is free to download, but then you pay for your own GPU.
  visual: bars — SORTING 10,000 EMAILS · LIST PRICE
[SPEED · Speed: too close to call] Cloudflare says Clef-flash answers in 39 milliseconds and Jev in 524. But Cloudflare tested Jev over the internet and its own model on its own servers. An independent test put Clef-flash at about 200 milliseconds, and TypeSafe says Jev takes 70 to 500. Both are fast.
  on screen: CLOUDFLARE'S TEST: 39 ms vs 524 ms (not fair) | CLEF-FLASH, INDEPENDENT: ~200 ms | JEV, TYPESAFE'S CLAIM: 70–500 ms
[ACCURACY · Split decision] On Cloudflare's 41 tests, Clef-flash won 24, Jev won 16, and one was a tie. But Jev won the hardest reasoning tests, like graduate-level science. And every one of these results comes from Cloudflare itself.
  visual: bars — TESTS WON (OF 41) · CLOUDFLARE'S RUN
[VERDICT · Pick by your need] So which one? Clef-flash if you want it free to download, need images, or must keep data on your own servers. Jev for the lowest price and harder judgment calls. Working in Telugu? Test both first. Neither publishes Telugu scores.
  on screen: PICK CLEF-FLASH: free download · images · own servers | PICK JEV: lowest price · harder calls | TELUGU?: test both first
