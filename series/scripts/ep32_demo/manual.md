# Chapter 1: Getting started

## 1. What's in the box
Your K7 box contains the phone, a USB-C cable, a SIM ejector pin, a clear back cover and a quick start leaflet. A charger is not included in the box; any USB-C charger rated 5V/2A or higher works, and the K7 charges fastest with a 33W charger. Keep the box and the invoice: you need the invoice for warranty claims.

## 2. Inserting SIM cards
Push the ejector pin into the small hole on the left edge to open the tray. The tray holds two nano-SIM cards, or one nano-SIM and one microSD card of up to 512 GB. Place the cards with the gold contacts facing down and push the tray back in until it clicks. Do not insert a SIM card while the phone is charging.

## 3. Turning the phone on and off
To switch the phone on, press and hold the power key on the right edge for three seconds until the logo appears. To switch it off, press and hold the power key and the volume-up key together, then tap Power off. To restart a frozen phone, hold the power key for 15 seconds.

## 4. First-time setup
When you switch on the K7 for the first time, choose your language, connect to Wi-Fi and sign in with your account. You can skip any step and finish it later in Settings. Setup also asks you to create a screen lock; we recommend a six-digit PIN.

## 5. Moving data from your old phone
Open the Switch app on both phones and keep them close together. The K7 can copy contacts, photos, videos, messages and most app data over a direct Wi-Fi link, so no internet connection is needed. Chat apps keep their own backups: back up your chats in the chat app on the old phone first, then restore them when you sign in on the K7. A full transfer of 50 GB takes about 40 minutes.

# Chapter 2: Battery and charging

## 6. Charging the battery
Connect the USB-C cable to the phone and to a wall charger. The battery icon shows a lightning bolt while charging. A full charge from empty takes about 75 minutes with a 33W charger and about 3 hours with a 10W charger. You can use the phone while it charges.

## 7. Fast charging and heat
The K7 supports 33W fast charging. It is normal for the phone to feel warm while fast charging, especially while playing games or using the camera at the same time. If the battery temperature goes above 45°C, the phone slows down charging automatically until it cools. Remove thick covers and avoid charging under pillows or blankets.

## 8. Battery saver
Battery saver limits background activity, lowers screen brightness and pauses automatic app updates. Turn it on from the quick settings panel, or set it to switch on by itself when the battery drops to 15%. Ultra battery saver keeps only calls, messages and up to six apps running and can stretch the last 10% of charge to about five hours.

## 9. Battery health and lifespan
Every battery wears out slowly. The K7 battery is designed to keep at least 80% of its original capacity after 800 full charge cycles, which is roughly two to three years of normal use. Check the current state in Settings > Battery > Battery health. Turning on Protect battery stops charging at 85% and helps the battery last longer.

## 10. Wireless charging
The K7 does not support wireless charging. Placing it on a wireless charging pad will not charge it and can make the back of the phone warm. Use the USB-C port to charge.

## 11. Charging safety
Use only cables and chargers that are certified and in good condition. Do not charge the phone if the port is wet or dusty; let it dry for at least an hour first. Never use a damaged cable, and do not leave the phone charging on a bed or sofa for long periods.

# Chapter 3: Display and sound

## 12. Screen timeout
To change how long the screen stays on when you are not touching it, go to Settings > Display > Screen timeout and choose from 15 seconds to 10 minutes. Turn on Stay awake while looking to keep the screen on while the front camera sees you reading.

## 13. Brightness and eye comfort
Adaptive brightness adjusts the screen to the light around you. Eye comfort mode adds a warm tint that reduces blue light; schedule it from sunset to sunrise in Settings > Display. Dark mode turns most backgrounds black and can save battery.

## 14. Ringtones and volume
Press the volume keys to change the volume of whatever is playing. To choose a ringtone, go to Settings > Sound > Phone ringtone. You can set a different ringtone for each SIM card and for each contact.

## 15. Do Not Disturb
Do Not Disturb silences calls, messages and notification sounds. Turn it on from quick settings, or schedule it, for example from 10 pm to 7 am. You can allow calls from starred contacts, and a second call from the same number within three minutes always rings.

## 16. Text size and display size
If the text is too small to read comfortably, go to Settings > Display > Font size and drag the slider. Display size makes buttons and icons bigger too. Magnification lets you zoom any part of the screen by tapping three times.

# Chapter 4: Camera

## 17. Taking photos
Open the Camera app and tap the shutter button, or press a volume key. Tap anywhere on the screen to focus there. The main camera has a 50 MP sensor; photos are saved at 12 MP by default, which gives sharper results in most light.

## 18. Night mode
Night mode takes several shots in a row and combines them into one brighter, cleaner photo. It switches on automatically in dark scenes; hold the phone still for about three seconds while the moon icon is shown. For the best results, rest the phone on something steady.

## 19. Meteor shower mode
Meteor shower mode is for photographing the night sky. Put the phone on a tripod, point it at a dark part of the sky and tap Start; the camera takes a long exposure of up to four minutes and records the streaks of shooting stars. Turn off the screen flash and avoid city lights.

## 20. Portrait mode
Portrait mode blurs the background behind a person so they stand out. Stay between half a metre and two metres from your subject. After taking the photo, you can change the blur strength in the Gallery app.

## 21. Recording videos
Swipe to Video in the Camera app and tap the red button. The K7 records up to 4K at 30 frames per second. Video stabilisation reduces shaking while you walk, and you can take a photo during recording by tapping the white circle.

## 22. Where photos are saved
Photos and videos are saved to the phone's internal storage, in the DCIM folder, and appear in the Gallery app. To save them to a microSD card instead, open Camera settings > Storage location. You can also turn on cloud backup so copies are kept online.

# Chapter 5: Connections

## 23. Wi-Fi
Go to Settings > Wi-Fi, turn it on and tap a network name to connect. The K7 supports Wi-Fi 6 on 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks. To share a saved network with a friend, tap it and show the QR code.

## 24. Mobile hotspot
Mobile hotspot shares your mobile data with other devices such as a laptop or tablet. Go to Settings > Connections > Hotspot, set a name and a password, and turn it on. Up to eight devices can connect at once. Hotspot uses battery quickly, so turn it off when you are done.

## 25. Bluetooth
Turn on Bluetooth from quick settings, then go to Settings > Bluetooth > Pair new device and choose your earphones, speaker or watch. The K7 remembers paired devices and reconnects to them automatically.

## 26. Using two SIM cards
The K7 supports dual SIM with dual standby: both numbers can receive calls, but only one can be on a call at a time. Choose which SIM is used for mobile data, calls and messages in Settings > SIM manager. Each SIM can be turned off without removing it.

## 27. Tap to pay (NFC)
The K7 has NFC, so you can pay at shop counters by holding the back of the phone near a card machine. Add a card in your payment app and set it as the default. NFC must be on, and the phone must be unlocked for payments above the limit set by your bank.

## 28. Airplane mode
Airplane mode turns off calls, mobile data, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth in one step. You can turn Wi-Fi and Bluetooth back on while staying in airplane mode, for example to use in-flight Wi-Fi.

# Chapter 6: Security

## 29. Fingerprint unlock
The fingerprint sensor is in the power key. Go to Settings > Security > Fingerprint and touch the key several times with different parts of your finger. You can add up to five fingerprints.

## 30. Face unlock
Face unlock uses the front camera to recognise your face. It is quick but less secure than a fingerprint or PIN, because a photo or a person who looks like you may unlock it. Face unlock cannot be used for payments.

## 31. Forgot your PIN or pattern
If you forget your screen lock, enter a wrong PIN five times and tap Forgot PIN to unlock with your account password. If that is not possible, the only option is a factory reset, which erases everything on the phone. Back up regularly so you never lose your data this way.

## 32. Find my phone
If you lose your phone, sign in at the Find My Device website from any browser. You can see the phone's location on a map, make it ring at full volume for five minutes even if it is on silent, lock it, or erase it. The phone must be switched on and connected to the internet.

## 33. Software updates
The K7 gets security updates every month and major software updates for three years from launch. Updates download over Wi-Fi and install when you restart. Keep at least 20% battery and 5 GB of free storage before updating.

# Chapter 7: Care and durability

## 34. Water and dust resistance
The K7 is rated IP54: it is protected against dust and light splashes, such as rain or spilled tea. It is not waterproof. Do not take it into the shower, the bathroom while bathing, a swimming pool or the sea, and never put it under running water. If the phone gets wet, dry it with a soft cloth and do not charge it until the port is completely dry.

## 35. Cleaning the phone
Wipe the screen with a soft, slightly damp microfibre cloth. Do not use window cleaner, alcohol sprays or rough cloths, which can damage the screen coating. Clean the USB-C port gently with a dry, soft brush.

## 36. Drops and screen protectors
The front glass is strengthened, but a fall onto a hard floor can still crack it. Use the clear cover from the box and a tempered glass screen protector to lower the risk of damage. A cracked screen should be replaced soon, because glass splinters can cut your fingers.

## 37. Operating temperature
The K7 works best between 0°C and 35°C. In freezing weather the battery may drain faster and the phone may switch off early; keep it in an inside pocket. Do not leave it in a parked car in the sun, where temperatures can go above 60°C.

# Chapter 8: Warranty and service

## 38. Warranty period
The K7 comes with a 12-month warranty from the date on your invoice. The battery and the in-box accessories, such as the cable, are covered for 6 months. Register your phone in the Support app to check your warranty status.

## 39. What the warranty does not cover
The warranty covers manufacturing defects only. It does not cover cracked or broken screens, liquid damage, damage from dropping the phone, or repairs done by unauthorised shops. These repairs can still be done at a service centre for a fee.

## 40. Returns and replacements
If the phone has a manufacturing defect, you can get a replacement within 7 days of purchase. Phones without a defect cannot be returned once they are opened. The phone must be in its original box with all accessories and the invoice.

## 41. Service centres and repair time
Find your nearest authorised service centre in the Support app. Most repairs are finished within 5 working days; screen replacements are often done the same day if the part is in stock. You will get an SMS when your phone is ready.

## 42. Before you hand over your phone for repair
Back up your photos, contacts and chats before a repair, because some repairs require a factory reset. Remove your SIM and microSD cards, and turn off Find My Device so the technician can test the phone.

## 43. Recycling your old phone
Do not throw old phones or batteries in the household bin. Take them to an authorised e-waste collection point or a service centre, where they are recycled safely. Erase your data with a factory reset first.

## 44. Safety information
Keep the phone at least 15 cm away from a pacemaker or other implanted medical device, and do not carry it in a chest pocket if you have one. Switch it off in hospitals where you are asked to, and do not use it while driving.

## 45. Contacting support
Call the helpline number printed on your invoice (toll-free, 9 am to 9 pm, every day) or chat with us in the Support app. Have your phone's IMEI number ready; you can find it by dialling *#06#.
