# Skit 05 "The client list" (what you paste into a free chatbot isn't private), script v1

Creative reel 05 (daily run 2, Oct 21, 2026). Format: an office skit with myth-vs-fact cards, loops. Cast: Lakshmi (team
lead, mid-thirties, kurta, glasses, office ID on a lanyard), Arjun (new joiner, 22, formal shirt, ID card), Sneha (new
joiner, last beat). Setting: an open-plan office in Hyderabad, laptops, a notice board ("TEAM LUNCH FRIDAY",
"Happy birthday Ramesh"). The chatbot is only text on the laptops; no brand on screen.

One idea a viewer can repeat to a friend: free chatbots can use your chats to improve the AI and some chats are read by
people, so swap names and numbers out before you paste; paste only what you'd pin on the notice board.

## Facts (checked Oct 21, 2026)
- Google, Gemini Apps Privacy Hub (support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961, "Last updated: October 19, 2026"):
  "Please don't enter confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see"; "A subset of chats are
  reviewed by human reviewers"; reviewed chats "are not deleted when you delete your activity" and are kept for up to
  three years; "To stop your future chats from being reviewed to improve Google services, turn off your Keep Activity
  setting"; chats with Keep Activity off and temporary chats are kept 72 hours and not used to train Google's models.
- OpenAI help center (Temporary Chat FAQ, Data Controls): saved chats follow the "Improve the model for everyone" setting
  (Settings > Data Controls); a temporary chat is not used to improve the models and a copy may be kept up to 30 days
  for safety. (help.openai.com refused our fetch; the wording is from the help center's search snippets and a
  university guide, BYU "ChatGPT privacy, model training and more".)
- Office accounts: OpenAI's business-data page: "We don't train our models on your organization's data by default"
  (ChatGPT Enterprise, Business, Edu, API). Google Workspace with Gemini: business data "is not reviewed by humans or used
  for generative AI model training outside your domain without permission" (workspace.google.com/security/ai-privacy).
  (Caption only.)

## Hook options
1. Lakshmi's hand slams Arjun's laptop lid half shut; the screen shows a chatbot box with "CLIENTS_FINAL.xlsx: 200
   names, phone numbers" and the cursor on SEND: "Arjun, STOP! Don't press send!" (kept: action and a shout in frame 1,
   a question by 3 s: why not?)
2. A chatbot reply fills the screen, "Here's your neat list: 200 clients with phone numbers", and Lakshmi behind him:
   "You gave our clients' numbers to... whom?" (slower: the stakes need reading)
3. A MYTH card first, "What you type to a chatbot is private", with a stamp. (a quiz, no story in frame 1)
