# Creative 42 "₹18,400 and a Date" (Nov 27 creative slot)

Format: **myth vs fact** (last used by Creative 35, Nov 20; the Nov 27 return agreed with Bujji). One narrator, no
dialogue. Cast (new): Kiran (26, first job, made up by us) and his phone. Frame 1 opens on a real amount the viewer could
lose (Bujji, Creative 28 7-day: "a payment, a document or a deadline"); our test's verdict before 10 s (Creative 28:
"put the verdict before 10 s; the three-myth count structure is a lecture"). Point family **I. Out of date: an AI's
answer has a date** (last used by Creative 27, Nov 12); the post before it in the queue is UC04 (T).
Time-sensitive: the date is 31 Dec 2026, so it must post before mid-December.

## Facts (series/scripts/creative42_demo/README.md)
- Our test: Qwen2.5-7B (4-bit), on our computer, no web search, 3 ways of asking, 10 fresh chats each, read by hand.
  The right answer (belated return by 31 Dec 2026, fee ₹5,000 / ₹1,000): **0 of 30**. Plain ask: 10/10 answered for FY
  2022-23. "I missed July": "no fixed deadline" 2/10. Told today's date: end of September, 31 Oct, 31 Mar 2027, or no
  date. An invented percentage fee ("0.5% of the tax a day, up to 25%") in 9/30.
- The Income Tax Department's e-filing help (AY 2026-27 FAQ): a belated return "may be furnished on or before 31st December
  2026"; fee ₹5,000, or ₹1,000 if total income is up to ₹5 lakh; an updated return "cannot result in ... increase in
  refund"; after 31 Dec a refund needs a condonation request (section 119(2)(b)), which can be refused.
- Kiran and his ₹18,400 refund are made up; the test and the rules are real. Apps with web search were not tested.

## Hook options
1. **Chosen: the refund and the AI's "no deadline".** Frame 1: Kiran on his sofa, phone lit with the test's real line
   "There is no fixed deadline for filing late returns after July 31"; blocks "REAL LAST DATE: 31 DEC" (green) vs "THE AI
   SAID: NO DEADLINE" (red); chip "KIRAN'S REFUND: ₹18,400". Line 1: "Kiran's eighteen-thousand-rupee refund has a last
   date: December thirty-first. The AI told him: no fixed deadline."
2. The count clash: "REAL LAST DATE: 31 DEC" vs "THE AI GOT IT: 0 OF 30": proven shape, but no person's money in it.
3. The old-year clash: "THE AI: 31 AUG 2023" vs "REAL: 31 DEC 2026": two dates, harder to feel.
