# UC04 "1 Video, 9 of 10": how YouTube and Netflix recommend videos (queue 067)

A teaching skit in Ravi anna's shop (ROUTINE.md Run 1). Cast: Ravi anna and the shop's phone AI. Ravi watches the
trading video a friend sent between customers; Up next fills with "get rich" trading. Topic (SYLLABUS UC04): embeddings
of you and of videos, similarity, ranking. Point family **T. It shows you what is close, not what you want** (new); the
posts before it are Creative 41 (B) and HOW TO 06 (F). The one variable (Bujji's EP57 48h read: "the leak is frame 1;
spend the next test there"): frame 1 is a count clash ("1 VIDEO" vs "UP NEXT: 9 OF 10 TRADING") instead of UC03's money
clash; the middle and length stay as UC03 (no quiz card, English about 55 s).

## Facts (series/scripts/uc04_demo/README.md)
- Our test: a small recommender on our computer, built on the idea in Google's 2016 YouTube paper (every video a list of
  numbers; "you" = the average of your watch history); 144 made-up video titles, Ravi's made-up history of 12.
  - Up next while the friend's video plays (the 10 closest to it): **9/10 trading**.
  - Autoplay, 10 in a row: **8/10 trading**.
  - His home page after those 10: **0/10** (averaged) or **2/10** (recent videos counted more). After the friend's video
    alone: 0/10 (2/10).
  - Up next while a warning video plays ("Trading tip scams: how fake gurus take your money"): **9/10 trading tips**.
- YouTube's own page: Up next "suggests additional content based on what you're currently watching"; signals:
  "viewing behaviour, likes, dislikes, subscriptions and feedback, including from satisfaction surveys".
- YouTube Help: "Not interested" (More, next to a video on Home or Watch next), "Don't recommend channel", remove videos
  from watch history, pause watch history.
- Netflix's help page: viewing history, ratings, "other members with similar tastes", title info, time of day, devices,
  how long you watch; recent outweighs past; no age or gender.

## Hook options
1. **Chosen: the count clash.** Frame 1: Ravi's phone, a made-up video app (no real logo): the friend's video playing,
   and the Up next list under it, nine red "₹" thumbnails ("₹1 lakh in one day", "Earn ₹10,000 a day") and one bhajan;
   two blocks: "HE WATCHED: 1 VIDEO" (blue) vs "UP NEXT: 9 OF 10 'GET RICH' TRADING" (red). Ravi: "One video my friend
   sent, and nine of the next ten are get-rich trading!"
2. Money first: "₹5,000 → ₹50,000 IN A WEEK?" thumbnail vs "his savings: ₹40,000": a stake, but no AI number.
3. The twist first: "HE WATCHED: A SCAM WARNING · UP NEXT: 9 TRADING TIPS": the best line, but it spends the ending.

## Script review (human-viewer, v1) and what changed
- Both: the hook and the scam twist work; line 6 is the weak spot (a lecture; 0 on screen vs "two" in the voice; "23";
  "at most") -> one card with both lists side by side, "UP NEXT: 9 OF 10 · follows the video on screen" vs "HOME: 2 OF 10
  · mixes in your whole history", and the voice says only the two numbers it shows (2/10 = our test with recent videos
  counted more, as Netflix's page says it does; the plain average's 0/10 goes to the caption). The rule aimed at the wrong
  list and missed autoplay -> "tap Not interested right in Up next, and switch autoplay off" (YouTube Help: Not interested
  works on Watch next; autoplay: "At the top of the video player, tap the Autoplay switch"). The twist and the home card
  need "our test" on screen -> chips on both.
- Sandeep: frame 1 has too much text -> right block "UP NEXT" / "9 OF 10 TRADING", the chip small; "we built a small one,
  a small what?" -> "We tested a small app like it"; fewer pops and ticks under the talking (one whoosh into the map, a
  short tick run on the lines, quiet ticks on the chain). Telugu: "చిన్నది" (heard as "a young girl") -> "ఇలాంటి చిన్న
  యాప్ తో"; "నాట్ ఇంట్రెస్టెడ్" could lose "నాట్" -> "నాట్ ఇంట్రెస్టెడ్, అంటే వద్దు, అని నొక్కాలి" with the button big;
  "ట్రేడింగేనా?!" as one word; line 1 ends "...డబ్బులు సంపాదించే వీడియోలే!"; "ప్లే అవుతున్న వీడియోకి"; "ఆటోప్లే ఆపేయాలి"
  (not "ఆఫ్", unclear before).
- Priya: story vs test blurred in frame 1 -> chip "Ravi is made up · this feed is our test app, not YouTube"; "close by
  what?" -> on the map, "our test: close = similar titles · YouTube's paper: learned from what people watch"; the twist is
  a title match -> its frame says "OUR TEST APP · it matches titles", and the caption says real apps may differ.
