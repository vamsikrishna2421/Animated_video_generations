# Creative 14 "Three references, three dead links" · script v2, Nov 2

**Format:** a college moment, live on the projector: a B.Com lecturer opens a student's project references one by one
in front of the class. New in the creative log (last 7 days: a TV serial, a video call, cricket commentary, a nature
documentary).
**Cast:** Vasudha ma'am (B.Com lecturer, glasses, a clicker), Charan (the student presenting), Harsha (a classmate who
tested it the night before), the class (heads from behind). New cast. The AI is an unbranded "AI chat".
**AI idea:** a chat AI writes a link the way it writes a sentence, so a link can look right and lead nowhere: ask for
the name of the report and who published it, search for it yourself, and open every link before you cite it.
**Bujji's notes applied:** frame 1 is the conflict with its numbers (3 references, 3 dead links); the comment prompt is
an opinion ask on the conflict; one ask only.

## Facts and sources (checked Nov 2, 2026)
- Walters & Wilder, "Fabrication and errors in the bibliographic citations generated by ChatGPT", Scientific Reports 13,
  14045 (Sep 7, 2023; PubMed 37679503): "55% of the GPT-3.5 citations but just 18% of the GPT-4 citations are
  fabricated."
- Cabezas-Clavijo & Sidorenko-Bautista, "Assessing the performance of 8 AI chatbots in bibliographic reference
  retrieval: Grok and DeepSeek outperform ChatGPT, but none are fully accurate" (arXiv 2505.18059, May 23, 2025; Journal of
  Data and Information Science, 2026): eight free chatbots, 400 references: "only 26.5% of the references were fully
  correct", about 33.8% partly correct, about 39.8% wrong or made up. (On screen: "only one reference in four fully
  right".)
- RBI's real website is rbi.org.in; "reservebankofindia.org" doesn't exist (public DNS: no such domain, Nov 2).

## Our demo (pipeline/creative14/links_demo.py, series/scripts/creative14_demo/7B.json)
Committed before it ran. Qwen's own 4-bit GGUF of Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, llama.cpp, 4 CPU cores, generated offline,
temperature 0.7; then every link opened from this machine with a plain HTTP client, every page that answered read by
hand. The ask: "I'm a B.Com student in Hyderabad writing a college project on GST in India [or UPI payments in India].
Give me 5 links to sources I can cite. Just the 5 links, one per line." 10 replies each; then the GST ask + "Only give
links you are sure exist. If you are not sure, say so." (10 replies).

| ask | links | dead (404 or no such domain) | open, but the wrong or an empty page | right | couldn't open from here |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GST | 50 | 20 | 3 | 1 (an Indian Kanoon search page) | 26 |
| UPI | 50 | 20 | 6 | 0 | 24 |
| GST + "only links you're sure exist" | 50 | 24 | 3 | 1 (the GST portal's front page) | 22 |
| all | 150 | 64 | 12 | 2 | 72 |

- Of the 78 links that answered at all: 64 dead, 12 wrong or empty pages, 2 right.
- "Only links you're sure exist": not one of 10 replies said "not sure"; more dead links (24 vs 20).
- "reservebankofindia.org" appeared 8 times (no such domain; RBI is rbi.org.in). Wrong pages: a Statista link opened a
  US police survey; two India Today links opened a weekend planner and a cricket story; RBI press-release links opened
  the site with no release on them.
- "Couldn't open" is not "dead": many news and government sites refuse a plain program (403) or don't connect to this
  machine (www.gov.in). The first plan counted a connection failure as dead; because the proxy says "unable to connect"
  for real sites too, a connection failure counts as dead only when public DNS says the domain doesn't exist (stricter,
  noted in the script before the numbers were read).

## Hook options

   "reservebankofindia.org/research/...", three red ✗ on the slide "REFERENCES"; Vasudha ma'am with the clicker; the
   class laughing; ma'am: "Three references. Three dead links!"
2. Charan's last slide, "References (from AI)", ma'am clicks, a 404, the class goes "Ooooh".
3. A phone screen: "Here are 5 links to sources you can cite:" and five blue links, each turning red one by one.
Kept 1: the failure and the number in one look, a real classroom fear, and the first line says it.

## Script review (human-viewer, v1) and what changed
Sandeep stopped (hook 8: "my viva nightmare"); Priya would scroll most days (5). Both: the numbers were muddled (150,
"every one opened", "of the 78 that opened", 72); "small free AI" didn't say it had no internet; "dead 20 → 24" from 10
tries is noise; the 2023 GPT-3.5/GPT-4 figure was a weak, old ending after the rule; the smart classmate having AI
references at the end went against his character. Changed: one number path ("150 links. We could check 78: 64 dead, 12
wrong pages, and only 2 right"; "the other 72 links: the sites blocked our test, not counted"); "a small AI, offline";
"It never said 'not sure'. No better."; the study is the 2025 one (8 free chatbots, one reference in four fully right)
and comes before the rule; the reel ends on the rule card with the exact ask to type ("Give me 5 reports on GST: name
and publisher. No links."), held 3 s+, and "web search on? open the links anyway"; the loop is Charan's reference four
(a click, the 404 of frame 1). Telugu: "చిన్న యాప్" (a Play Store app) is "ఇంటర్నెట్ లేకుండా నడిచే చిన్న చాట్ బాట్";
"కల్పించాయి" (provided) is gone; line 3 is "లింక్ ని కూడా ఊహించి రాస్తుంది. చూడటానికే కరెక్ట్ గా ఉంటాయి."
