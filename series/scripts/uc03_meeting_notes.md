# UC03 "$14.50 a Bag": how AI meeting notes work (queue 066)

A teaching skit in Ravi anna's shop (ROUTINE.md Run 1). Cast: Ravi anna and the shop's phone AI, which now takes notes of
his supplier calls (made up; our test is real). Topic (SYLLABUS UC03): speech-to-text → who spoke → summary → action
items. Point family **P** (what it heard is not what you said), last used by EP56 (Nov 18); the posts before it are UC02
(M) and Creative 39 (D). The one variable (Bujji's Creative 32 48h read): a one-beat middle (the ask, the miss, the fix),
English 55 s or less, instead of UC01/UC02's steps-first middle with a quiz.

## Facts
- Our test (`series/scripts/uc03_demo/README.md`; both scripts committed before they ran): a made-up 8-turn supplier
  call (40 bags at "fourteen fifty", ₹1,450 a bag; ₹14,000 advance), two computer voices. Speech-to-text on our computer
  wrote "fourteen fifty a bag" as "$14 .50 a bag" and "fourteen thousand" as "$14 ,000"; with "rupees" said, "14 .50
  rupees"; with a hint ("prices are in Indian rupees"), words, no dollar sign. A small free AI wrote the notes, 10 fresh
  chats each: $14.50 a bag in 7/10, the order as "$580" in 7/10 (the real order: ₹58,000). Who keeps the 3 empty sacks
  ready (Ravi): no names on the turns, 6/10 said Suresh; names on the turns, 10/10 Ravi.
- Google Meet "Take notes for me" (Google's help page): English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese,
  Spanish; one language per meeting; "Sometimes, the meeting summary can be incomplete, inaccurate, or not generated."
  Zoom's meeting summary (Zoom's "Supported languages for Zoom AI features"): Hindi and Telugu are listed. Not tested.

## Hook options
1. **Chosen: the same price, two currencies.** Frame 1: blocks "THE CALL: ₹1,450 A BAG" (green) vs "AI NOTES: $14.50 A
   BAG" (red); chip "40 BAGS: ₹58,000 → $580". Ravi: "On the call: fourteen fifty a bag. In the AI notes: fourteen dollars
   fifty!"
2. Who-does-what clash: "NO NAMES: SURESH KEEPS THE SACKS 6/10" vs "WITH NAMES: RAVI 10/10": true, but no money.
3. The notes page itself with a red circle: too much text for frame 1.

## On screen (English in both versions)
- Frame 1: two blocks over the shop, "THE CALL: ₹1,450 A BAG" (green) vs "AI NOTES: $14.50 A BAG" (red); chip "40 BAGS:
  ₹58,000 → $580"; Ravi with the notes on his phone.
- Line 2 (one card, the whole middle): three boxes, EARS (the call as text, "...$14 .50 a bag..." circled red), WHO
  SPOKE ("Ravi: / Suresh:" on the lines), NOTES ("$14.50 a bag · total $580"); "7/10 notes copied the dollars"; chip
  "Ravi and the shop are made up · our test is real".
- Line 4: two note cards side by side: "NO NAMES ON THE TURNS: 'Suresh: keep 3 empty sacks' 6/10" (red) vs "NAMES ON THE
  TURNS: 'Ravi: keep 3 empty sacks' 10/10" (green).
- Line 5: a help-page card: "Google Meet notes: 8 languages, no Telugu · 'can be incomplete, inaccurate'" / "Zoom meeting
  summary: Telugu, Hindi listed" · "their own help pages · we didn't test them".
- Line 6: stamps "NOTES ARE A DRAFT" / "CHECK EVERY NUMBER · EVERY NAME"; a chip for app makers: "tell speech-to-text
  'prices in rupees': no dollar sign".
- Line 7: back to frame 1 (loops).

Sound: a cash-register clang and a buzz on frame 1; pops on the three boxes, a hit on "$14 .50"; a drop before "six of
ten"; a buzz and a success on the two note cards; a lift into the rule; a riser and the slam back to frame 1.
Caption try-it (no comment ask): "Next time an app writes your call notes, read every number and every name before you
forward them."

## Script review (human-viewer, v1) and what changed
- Both: "$580" fell flat ($580 is about ₹50,000) → the "rupees" result is the hook: with "rupees" said, the ears wrote
  "14 .50 rupees", and the third test (`notes_demo3.py`, committed before it ran) shows the notes then say "14.50 rupees
  a bag" in 7/10 and the order "580 rupees" in 5/10: rice at ₹14.50 a bag. Line 2 had too much → the steps, then the
  miss, "we ran it ten times". The Meet/Zoom beat felt untested and off-topic → out of the voice, into the caption.
- Priya: "names fix who, not money" said; "a small free AI on our computer · names added by us · 10 runs" on screen;
  the hint fix shown end to end → tested: the dollar sign went, but 8/10 notes still wrote 14.50 (caption only); the
  notes2.json auto-label for seeds 3 and 9 footnoted in the README.
- Sandeep: "turns" is jargon → "who said what"; one tip a viewer can do → tested: the amount said in full ("one
  thousand four hundred and fifty rupees") came through right in every note that gave a price (7/10, 0 wrong). Telugu:
  "సురేష్ అని రాశాయి" (not "సురేష్ అన్నాయి"); the who-spoke step named; "చెక్ చేసుకోండి"; no lone "నోట్" (Creative 39:
  heard as "nood").
