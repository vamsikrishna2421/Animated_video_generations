# Creative 08 "Ask me first" (script v3 as rendered, Oct 24)

**Format:** "Take 1 / Take 2": the same request to an AI, done wrong, rewound (VHS rewind, a TAKE 2 slate), done right.
Two-character skit on a terrace at night, phone-chat inserts, a test card, loops.
**Cast / setting:** Kavya (24, works in an office, the planner) and her brother Teja (17, dramatic) on their building's
terrace at night (fairy lights, the water tank, a clothesline); Amma and Nanna in the payoff. The AI is an unbranded
chat app ("AI chat") on Kavya's phone.
**AI idea:** an AI that knows nothing about you fills the gaps with a guess (here: the generic big party). You often
don't know which details it needs, so add one line, "Before you plan, ask me 3 questions," and it asks first.
Sources: PCWorld (Ben Patterson, Mar 23 2026: "Ask me 5 clarifying questions first"; ChatGPT and Claude sometimes ask on
their own, Gemini needed the line), Tom's Guide (Amanda Caswell, Jul 8 2026: chatbots "don't know they're missing"
details, "they simply make their best guess"; Jun 4 2026: "ask up to three clarifying questions"). Our own test on a
small free AI offline (pipeline/creative08/plan_demo.py, every reply read by hand) gives the card's numbers and the
AI's three questions.
**Why it travels:** every family has planned a parents' anniversary; a 3-lakh plan for a dad who won't take an auto is a
relatable laugh; the fix is one sentence anyone can copy.
