# EP48 "AI laws: EU AI Act, India and the rest" (Season 5: Responsible AI) · queue 051 · script v3 (as rendered), Nov 8

## The lesson
A chat AI learns the world up to a date. Laws keep moving after it: India's AI-label rules came into force on
20 Feb 2026; the EU banned some AI uses from Feb 2025 and moved its own high-risk dates this July. So an AI's answer about
a law is at best as old as its training, and sometimes simply wrong in a confident voice. For any law question: ask the
AI "as of what date?", then check the government's own page and its date. And the rule itself: an AI-made video, photo
or voice that looks real needs a clear label.

## Facts and sources (checked Nov 8, 2026)
- India: Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026, notified by
  MeitY on 10 Feb 2026, in force 20 Feb 2026 (AZB & Partners: "The Amendment Rules will come into force on February 20,
  2026."; Freshfields; Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer (hlc.com); all three agree). "Synthetically generated information"
  = AI-made or altered audio, visual or audio-visual content that appears real; "text generated by AI does not fall within
  SGI" (Freshfields). It must be "clearly and prominently labelled" (no fixed size: the draft's 10% rule was dropped);
  permanent metadata where feasible. Big platforms (over 5 million users in India) must require users to declare whether
  an upload is AI-made, verify it, and label it (AZB, Freshfields). Unlawful content: taken down within 3 hours of a court
  order or a reasoned government notice (was 36 hours) (AZB, Freshfields). MeitY's own site refused our fetch (403).
- EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689: prohibited practices apply from 2 Feb 2025, including "emotion recognition in
  workplaces and education institutions" and "social scoring" (the Commission's own page,
  digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai; recital 179 on EUR-Lex). Generally applicable from
  2 Aug 2026 (Commission page).
- The Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 Jul 2026 (Commission page): rules for stand-alone
  high-risk systems (Annex III: hiring, credit, education...) "will apply from 2 December 2027" instead of 2 Aug 2026
  (Commission page; Orrick, Jul 29, 2026: "will now take effect on 2 December 2027 instead of 2 August 2026"); Annex I
  products 2 Aug 2028.
- Not used: the EU fine amounts (Article 99 not read on a primary page this run).
