# Creative 37 "5 Reasons, 1 Email" (Nov 22 creative slot)

Format: **story mode** (one calm storyteller, one character, simple pictures, a reveal). Last story mode: Creative 29
(Nov 14). Not a dialogue skit (Creative 36 was one). Point family **L** (AI writes for anyone, unless you give it you;
last used by 25 on Nov 10; 36 was B, EP58 was R). Cast: Ramya (new: 27, a support executive, a mustard kurti, long hair),
made up; Kiran (her manager) and Sravya (her teammate) appear only as names in an inbox. The test is ours:
`series/scripts/creative37_demo/README.md` (Qwen2.5-7B on our computer, 10 + 10 fresh chats, read by hand).

## Hook options (frame 1 + first line)
1. **Count clash + scarcity (chosen).** Frame 1: a dark bar "SAME FRIDAY · ONLY 2 CAN GO", two blocks "5 DIFFERENT
   REASONS" (green) and "1 SAME EMAIL" (red), an inbox of five rows that all read "...personal reasons". Line: "Five
   leave emails for the same Friday. Kiran can approve two." Count clash (Creative 31's proven shape, 66.8%) + the
   viewer's own leave at stake + an injustice (the real reason is buried).
2. Same-item clash: "HER REASON: SISTER'S ENGAGEMENT" vs "HER EMAIL: 'PERSONAL REASONS'". Clear, but one person's
   wording problem, no scarcity: weaker injustice.
3. Test first: "10 CHATS · 10 SAME FIRST LINE". True and sharp, but no person at stake (Creative 30's lesson).

## Script review (human-viewer, v1) and what changed
- Both: the hook never said the five used an AI; "tickets" read as train tickets; "invoice report" came from nowhere;
  "not ChatGPT" read as "not about my app"; the AI's own name in the sign-off is the laugh to keep.
- Priya: placeholders like "[Manager's Name]" are correct, not a failure; lead with made-up dates; "back Monday" was 9 of
  10, not 10; the facts run still guessed a month in all 10, the best case for "read before you send"; the "approved"
  ending must come from her facts and her check, not a better sob story; the drop belongs before the bad news.
- Sandeep: "only hers on hold?" (now all five); "who is Kiran?" (now "their manager"); Telugu: అడుగు (step/foot) out,
  "ఎవరికీ తెలియదు" sounded like a secret, తనదే pointed at Kiran, సపోర్ట్ టికెట్లు, ఎంగేజ్‌మెంట్ instead of నిశ్చితార్థం,
  పదింటిలోనూ, say the actual English line, "తన లీవ్ హోల్డ్ లో పడింది", "చెప్పడానికి ఇబ్బంది లేని కారణం".
- Frame 1 was crammed and had no "AI" on it: the right block now reads "THE AI WROTE · 1 EMAIL", the story chip moves off
  frame 1, the inbox rows are bigger.
- Big apps: not tested (no account or key here). On screen "a small free AI on our computer"; the caption's try-it asks
  the viewer to type the vague ask into their own app and read the first line.
