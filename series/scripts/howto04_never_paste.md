## The real run: the official pages, read Nov 12, 2026
Google, "Gemini Apps Privacy Hub" (support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961, updated Oct 26, 2026):
- "Human reviewers (including trained reviewers from our service providers) review some of the data"
- "Reviewed chats are retained for up to three years." (deleting your activity does not delete reviewed chats)
- "To stop your future chats from being reviewed to improve Google services, turn off your Keep Activity setting."
- "Temporary chats and chats you have when Keep Activity is off are retained with your account for 72 hours"
- "Temporary chats are not used to train Google's AI models." Default auto-delete: 18 months.
- "Please don't enter confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see"
OpenAI, "Data controls in ChatGPT" (help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893; read via search, the page refuses automated
fetches): data sharing ("Improve the model for everyone") is on by default for Free, Plus and Pro accounts; turning it
off stops new chats being used to train; Temporary Chats are not used to improve the models and may be kept up to 30
days for safety. openai.com and help.openai.com refuse automated fetches (403), so the help page was read through
search results; the menu path is confirmed by Yahoo Tech ("Keep your ChatGPT data private by opting out of training") and
ESET ("Is ChatGPT safe to use?"). The reel claims only the path, "on by default" and "temporary chats aren't used to train".
Anthropic, "Is my data used for model training?" (privacy.claude.com, Mar 16, 2026) and "Updates to our consumer
terms" (Aug 28, 2025): you choose whether chats are used to improve Claude ("Model Improvement" in Privacy Settings);
"extending data retention to five years, if you allow us to use your data for model training"; otherwise "30-day data
retention"; "Your Incognito chats are not used to improve Claude".
Microsoft, "Privacy FAQ for Microsoft Copilot": "By default, we store conversation activity for 18 months."; you can
"opt out of use of your conversation activity for model training"; "You shouldn't provide any confidential or sensitive
personal data that you would not want Microsoft to use".
Google, "Manage & delete your Gemini Apps activity" (support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892): on the web, "Settings &
help", then "Activity" (or myactivity.google.com/product/gemini); "Near the top, click On", then "Turn off"; "Even when
Keep Activity is off, your conversations will be saved with your account for up to 72 hours". Temporary chats
(answer/13275745): "Next to New chat, click Temporary chat."; they "won't appear in your Gemini Apps Activity or your
recent chats". The mobile app's steps aren't on these pages, so the reel shows the web path.

Meta AI: its privacy pages didn't say today whether Meta AI chats train its models or how to opt out, so the reel
leaves Meta AI out.
