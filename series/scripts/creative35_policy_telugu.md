# Creative 35 "30 Days or 30 Weeks?" · Nov 20 creative slot · script v3

## Format and rotation
- **Format:** myth vs fact, as agreed with Bujji for Nov 20. Last used by Creative 28 on Nov 13. One narrator, no
  dialogue.
- **Cast (new this week):** Sarojini garu (60, made up by us) and her phone.
- **Point family: new, Q "Lost in translation: the AI's Telugu changes the money".** Creative 34 (E) posts just before
  this one, so the families don't repeat.
- **Bujji's Creative 28 / 29 reads:**
  - The stake is the viewer's own money: a claim deadline and a hospital bill.
  - The viewer's own check comes before 10 s.
  - No ask built around posting a test result.

## Hook options (kept: A)
- **A.** Frame 1: "POLICY: CLAIM WITHIN 30 DAYS" against "THE AI'S TELUGU: 30 WEEKS", with "₹48,000 CLAIM" and a
  "REJECTED" stamp, and Sarojini garu with her phone. Line 1: "Claim within thirty days, says Sarojini garu's policy.
  The AI's Telugu said thirty weeks."
  - The stake is her money. The two numbers sit on the same line of the same policy. The injustice is that she trusted
    the AI.
- **B.** "'Not covered' survived zero times." Strong, but not a number clash.
- **C.** "Two years became two weeks." Good, but the deadline (A) is the viewer's own action.

## Our test (`series/scripts/creative35_demo/README.md`; every reply read by hand)
A small free AI on our computer, a 10-line policy we wrote, 5 chats per ask.
- **Usual ask:**
  - "30 days" was right 1 of 5 (also "30 weeks", "30 numbers").
  - "2 years" was right 1 of 5 (also "2 weeks").
  - "Not covered" was kept 0 of 5.
- **"Keep every number and every 'not'":**
  - "30 days" 4 of 5.
  - "2 years" 2 of 5.
  - "Not covered" 0 of 5.
- **Fact:** IRDAI's Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests (Sep 5, 2024): the Customer Information
  Sheet and the proposal form must be available in regional languages on request (Business Standard, Sep 6, 2024;
  TeamLease RegTech; Angel One).
- **Honest limits:** big apps (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google Translate) were not tested, and they do Telugu much better. The
  ₹48,000 claim and its rejection are our story. The test is real.

## Script review (human-viewer, v1), what changed for v2
Sandeep (24, job hunting), Priya (28, HR) and Lakshmi (52, Telugu, a policyholder) all stopped on frame 1.
- **The reel argued against itself:** "Myth: an AI can translate your policy" sat next to "big apps do Telugu better".
  We can't test Google Translate or ChatGPT from here, so the myth is now what our test showed and what holds for any
  app: "if the numbers match, the translation is right". The fact: the number stayed and the unit changed.
  "Not Google Translate" is said out loud.
- **"So what do I do?":** the action is exact. The voice says "Customer Information Sheet" and who to ask (your agent
  or customer care), and the rule card is a message to screenshot: "Please send my policy's Customer Information Sheet
  in Telugu." It also notes that the sheet is a summary and the policy wording stays the reference.
- **The story is labelled:** "Imagine" / "ఒకవేళ" opens line 1, and frame 1 carries "a story · our test is real".
- **Honest counts:** "once it said thirty weeks", "two years, once, became two weeks" (each happened in 1 of 5 replies).
- **The real flip is shown:** maternity "not covered" became "జనన ఖర్చులు పరిష్కరించబడతాయి" (birth expenses will be
  settled), as the model wrote it.
- **Frame 1:** only 30 DAYS against 30 WEEKS, Sarojini garu and the phone line "30 వారాల". The ₹48,000 bar lands at
  1.5 s; the REJECTED stamp lands on the word.
- **Telugu:** రెండు సంవత్సరాలు (రెండేళ్లు blurred), ట్రాన్స్‌లేషన్, డబ్బు పేపర్లకి, కస్టమర్ ఇన్ఫర్మేషన్ షీట్, ఇన్సూరెన్స్
