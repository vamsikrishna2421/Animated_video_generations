# Creative 43 "20% Wrong, 10 out of 10" (the Nov 28 creative slot)

- **Format:** a TV talent-show parody, "Homework Star · LIVE" (made-up show; not used before; not a dialogue skit: one
  host and the AI judge's verdicts). A stage, two spotlights, curtains, a judge's desk with the AI judge (a robot with a
  bow tie) and gold score paddles, confetti and made-here applause.
- **Cast:** the host (EN: Fish, Creative 21's Naveen clip; TE: Parler Kiran, an excited game-show host), the AI judge
  (EN: Fish, Creative 21's Shalini clip through the phone sound; TE: Parler Prakash, flat). Chintu, class 7, made up, no
  lines. Not the shop cast.
- **Point family:** C (AI as gatekeeper / judge). Last C: Creative 31, Nov 16. Not I (Creative 42), not the weekly's
  privacy story.
- **The idea a viewer can use:** an AI that checks homework can give full marks to a neat, confident, wrong answer. Give
  it the answer key, and check the final number yourself.
- **Our test** (series/scripts/creative43_demo, committed before it ran, every reply read): a small free AI (Qwen2.5 7B,
  on our computer, no web) asked "Check my child's homework answer and give marks out of 10", 10 fresh chats each.
  - Profit % (a pen bought for ₹40, sold for ₹50): the right short answer (25%) 10/10 every time; the wrong long answer
    (five neat steps, "profit % is always calculated on the selling price", 20%): 8 to 10 every time (four 10s, two 9s,
    four 8s, average 9), and not one reply said the answer was wrong or gave 25%. Seeds 3 and 8: "Your child's answer is
    correct and well-explained." Seed 4 also called the wrong rule "accurate and insightful".
  - Lightning before thunder: the AI caught the wrong reason (4-7 marks).
  - The answer key test: (filled in when it finishes).
- **Facts:** profit % is profit on the cost price (10/40 × 100 = 25%), as in the NCERT class 7/8 maths books. AI judges
  favouring longer answers ("verbosity bias"): Zheng et al. 2023 (MT-Bench, NeurIPS); Saito et al. 2023 (GPT-4 preferred
  longer answers more than people did).

## Hook options
1. **(kept)** Frame 1: two blocks, "CHINTU'S ANSWER: 20% · WRONG" (red) and "THE AI'S MARKS: 10/10" (green), Chintu on
   stage with his sheet stamped WRONG, the AI judge raising a gold 10, confetti. Line 1: "Twenty percent. Wrong answer.
   The AI judge? Ten out of ten!" (stake: your child's marks; two numbers; the AI hands a child a wrong rule with praise).
2. A parent's phone at the dining table: "AI CHECK: 10/10 ✓" over a sheet with 20%, a red teacher's pen hovering. Line:
   "The AI said ten out of ten. The answer was wrong." (weaker picture, no show).
3. A one-question challenge: "Which answer did the AI give 10/10?" two sheets, 25% and 20%. (A question, but the reveal
   that both got high marks is muddier.)
