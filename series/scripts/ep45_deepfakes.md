# EP45 "Deepfakes and how to spot them" (Season 5: Responsible AI) · queue 048 · script v3 (as rendered), Nov 4

## The lesson
A deepfake is a video where AI puts a real person's face and voice on words they never said; it learns them from the
person's public videos. Your eyes are a weak test (the new ones fix the old tells), so check two things that don't
depend on the picture: the money (no real person promises lakhs in a week) and the source (their own verified handle,
or the government's fact check). Paid already: 1930.

## The reach stack (Standing Rule, Nov 3; the variable stays the hook, built life-first as EP44)
1. Stake: Chinnu's own savings, ₹22,000 (his phone money), paid after a video.
2. Two clashing numbers in frame 1, readable with the sound off: PAID ₹22,000 vs PROMISED ₹5,50,000 in 1 WEEK.
3. Injustice: a famous face (fake) took a boy's savings. The AI-made video is the culprit; Amma catches it.
No likeness of any real person: the ad's face is hidden ("face hidden by us"), no real voice is imitated.

## Facts and sources (checked Nov 4, 2026)
- PIB Fact Check, Apr 16, 2026 (on X; reported by LatestLY, Morung Express, IBTimes India, Lokmat Times): a viral video
  of Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman claiming ₹22,000 could return ₹5.5 lakh within a week is fake and AI-generated:
  "Neither the Government nor the Finance Minister is endorsing any such investment scheme." All India Radio
  (newsonair.gov.in) reported further such debunks through 2026 (Jan, Apr, Jul, Aug).
- RBI press release 2024-2025/1537, Nov 19, 2024: fake videos of the Governor "claim launch of or support to some
  investment schemes"; "RBI does not give any such financial investment advice"; the public is "cautioned against
  engaging with and falling prey to such deepfake videos".
- MIT Media Lab, "Detect Fakes" project: check whether lip movements look natural and whether shadows fall where you'd
  expect; "there's no single tell-tale sign of how to spot a fake".
- MHA / I4C: report cyber financial fraud on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in (as skit 02).
