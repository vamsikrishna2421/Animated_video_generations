# EP33 recording sheet: "We don't sell headaches"

Two characters, both your voice. RAVI: loud, fast, gruff (even on calm lines). AI: calm, flat, a little slower, polite;
it gets a phone-speaker sound later.

How to record:
- Start with 5 seconds of silence.
- Say the line number in English, wait 1 second, say the line, wait 2 seconds.
- Slip? Pause and say the line again. The last take is used.
- ★ lines: say them twice. For the AI, the second take slower, with a longer pause.
- "E-1203" = "E twelve zero three".
- Quiet small room, fan/AC off, phone on Do Not Disturb, 20-30 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side.
- Send the original voice-recorder file (m4a or wav), not a WhatsApp voice note: attach it in the chat, or put it in
  Google Drive with "anyone with the link" and paste the link (sharing can be turned off once it's downloaded), or upload
  it on GitHub into `incoming/` on the `sample1` branch.
- English and Telugu as two separate files. Telugu is optional.
- Change words to how you would really say them; that's fine.

## English

**2** AI: E-1203 wasn't on the three pages I read.

**5** RAVI: A code has no meaning! It has to match exactly!

**6** AI: Then use keyword search. Exact words only.

**7** RAVI: Good. Only that from now on.

**8** AI: Next order: "something for a headache."

**9 ★** AI: No match. We don't sell headaches.

**10** RAVI: The pain balm is right there!

**11** AI: He never said "balm".

**12** RAVI: So which search do I use?!

**13** AI: Both. Merge the two lists. Still three pages.

**14** RAVI: And how do I know it works?

**15** AI: Test me like a new delivery boy.

**16** AI: Twenty orders you already know the answers to.

**17** AI: Meaning only: fifteen out of twenty. Keyword only: thirteen.

**18 ★** AI: Both: eighteen.

**19** RAVI: Eighteen. So two customers went home empty-handed. Which two?

**20** AI: "Something to wash vessels." And "Ramesh garu's usual."

**21** RAVI: Everyone knows those!

**22 ★** AI: Everyone except your stock book.

**23** RAVI: Written. Test again.

**24** AI: Twenty out of twenty.

**25** RAVI: Super! Why two? Switch one off.

**26 ★** AI: Okay. Keyword off. Next order: E-1203.
