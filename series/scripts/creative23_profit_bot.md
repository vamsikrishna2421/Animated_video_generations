# Creative 23 "Three Lakhs on the Screen" (the Nov 8 creative slot) · script v3 (as rendered), Nov 8

## The idea (point family G, scams dressed as AI; last G post: EP45, Nov 3)
"AI trading bot" is a costume. No AI, and no real investment, pays a fixed profit every day. The app's balance that
rises exactly 3% a day is a formula, not trading: ₹2,00,000 × 1.03, fifteen times = ₹3,11,593. If it were real, ₹2 lakh
would be about ₹970 crore in a year. The real product is the "tax" or "fee" it asks before you can withdraw. The tells:
a fixed daily profit, a fee to withdraw. What to do: don't pay; check the name on SEBI's site; call 1930 /
cybercrime.gov.in.

## Facts and sources (checked Nov 8, 2026)
- SEBI: "no guarantees of assured returns in the securities market" (investor caution, Feb 2024: Business Standard,
  Moneylife); its June 2022 caution (PR 20/2022) on unregulated platforms selling algo-trading strategies with claims of
  huge returns (Business Standard, Taxmann, Free Press Journal). Registration can be checked on SEBI's list of registered
  intermediaries (sebi.gov.in).
- The pattern in 2026 police cases: WhatsApp/Telegram groups, a fake trading app with a growing dashboard, a small early
  withdrawal for trust, then a "tax"/"fee" before any big withdrawal (Free Press Journal: Mumbai, Malad, Mar 2026: asked
  ₹5 lakh to "activate" an ₹18 lakh withdrawal; Mumbai, Prabhadevi: a 5% "tax"; Mumbai: the victim's chartered accountant
  told him no such tax is needed to withdraw; Indore: a "cross-country tax"; Etemaad Daily: Hyderabad, an "AI trading"
  scam). Press reports of police complaints.
- 1930: the national cyber-fraud helpline run by I4C (Ministry of Home Affairs) since April 2022, and
  cybercrime.gov.in (Kerala Police, India TV, Vikaspedia).
- Our numbers (arithmetic): 2,00,000 × 1.03^15 = 3,11,593; 18% of it = 56,087; 2,00,000 × 1.03^365 = 969.7 crore.
- Sastry garu, Pallavi and "AI Profit Bot" are made up (no real app or brand).
