# Skit 02 "The call in my own voice" — script v3 (as voiced, after the render review)

Format: a relatable home moment with an AI twist (one wide sitcom frame on the sofa + phone-screen inserts; not skit 01's
shot/reverse-shot). Cast: AMMA (~55, sharp), CHINNU (~22, her son, eating dosa), the CALLER (Chinnu's voice, cloned,
through the phone speaker). Setting: a Hyderabad living room, evening. Loops: the last frame is Amma answering the next
call, which is the first frame.

The real AI idea (verified): scammers copy a loved one's voice from short clips posted online and call with a fake
emergency; a family code word set offline, and hanging up to call back on a number you know, beat it.
- FBI IC3 PSA I-120324-PSA (Dec 3, 2024), "Criminals Use Generative Artificial Intelligence to Facilitate Financial
  Fraud": "Criminals generate short audio clips containing a loved one's voice to impersonate a close relative";
  "Create a secret word or phrase with your family to verify their identity"; "Verify the identity of the person
  calling you by hanging up the phone" and calling directly.
- FTC consumer alert (Mar 20, 2023), "Scammers use AI to enhance their family emergency schemes": a scammer needs "a
  short audio clip of your family member's voice — which he could get from content posted online"; "Call the person
  who supposedly contacted you and verify the story. Use a phone number you know is theirs." Red flags: wiring money,
  crypto, gift cards.
- India: report cyber financial fraud on helpline 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in (MHA / Indian Cyber Crime Coordination
  Centre; 1930 operational since April 2022, round the clock).

Hook options (first frame + first line):
1. KEPT: Amma's phone rings "Unknown number"; Chinnu sits right beside her, mid-bite. The phone, in Chinnu's voice:
   "Amma! It's me, Chinnu! I lost my phone, this is a friend's!" (something is wrong in frame 1: he is right there).
2. Black screen, a crying voice note: "Amma, I'm in trouble..." then the reveal that the son is in the room.
   (Slower: the wrong thing only lands at 3 s.)
3. Chinnu to camera: "My mom just got a call from me. I was sitting next to her." (A POV caption hook; tells, doesn't show.)
