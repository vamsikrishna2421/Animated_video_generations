# Storytelling notes (from reference reels)

## tomxsystems — "Claude just became a graphic designer" (plumber brand film, 56 s, music only)
https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd8_4iAua0A/

Structure (problem → stakes → solution → proof → brand):
1. **Motif first** (0-5 s): a pipe draws itself across tiles. The same pipe returns at the end and bends into the logo letter "B". One visual thread carries the whole film.
2. **Problem, felt not told**: "Every leak starts small." → a clock at 02:47 → "Water through the ceiling." Time + a relatable emergency, three beats, no narration.
3. **Turn**: a phone appears, the call is made → the product (website) slides in: "Fixed fast. Across London."
4. **Show the product working**: exploded 3D layers of the UI, services grid ("Six trades. One call."), a real use case ("Boiler down? Warm by tonight." + booking modal), a map with the van's route.
5. **Proof**: bars rise to "+312%", rating / jobs counters tick up.
6. **Brand**: the pipe returns and forms the logo, "Brunel & Co."

Craft rules to copy:
- Short headline pairs: bold sans + italic serif accent word ("Fixed fast. *Across London.*").
- Each scene grows out of the previous one (match cuts on shape/colour); nothing just fades in.
- One recurring motif that pays off in the last shot.
- Every 2-4 s a new idea; cuts land on the music.
- Real-looking UI and numbers (the shadcn / component-gallery point in RESOURCES.md).

## Applying it to movie narration
- Open on the movie's motif (an object/symbol), state the protagonist's problem in 2-3 relatable beats, then the turn.
- Narrate in everyday, relatable language (Telugu RJ style for the Telugu page), visuals carry the drama.
- Recurring motif → final payoff shot; end card with the brand.
